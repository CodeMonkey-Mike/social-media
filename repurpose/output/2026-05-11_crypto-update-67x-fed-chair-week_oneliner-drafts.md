## Notes on the format

These work better with images than long-form tweets do for two reasons:

1. **Single-sentence tweets without an image read as low-effort.** The image is what justifies the brevity.
2. **The image lets you skip the explanation paragraph.** The thesis is "implied by image, stated by text."

If any of these land for you, generate the matching image via `generate-image.js` (image direction notes above are starting points for the prompt), then add to `tweets.csv` with both `image_id` and `image_path` populated. Same image can cross-post to `single-image-IG.json` for IG.
