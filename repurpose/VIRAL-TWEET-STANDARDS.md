### B) Writing Principles Cheat Sheet (drop into Claude knowledge base)

1. **Specificity beats adjectives.** Use exact numbers ("$70T," "+5.78%," "60 agents in 60 hours") not "huge" or "many." Two-decimal precision is an authority marker.
2. **Hook lives in the first 7 words.** Lead with a verb, a number, a name, or a contradiction. Never bury it.
3. **One thought per line.** Triple-line stat parallelism is the most screenshotted format on CT. Use line breaks aggressively.
4. **Withhold the punchline.** Set up the data, then deliver the conclusion in a final short line ("more than half," "Let that sink in," "This is why we bitcoin").
5. **Coin a phrase.** Named ideas ("Americanization of Crypto," "enshrined L2," "Bitcoin tourists") get repeated. Repetition is virality.
