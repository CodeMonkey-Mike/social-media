Volumes are deliberately low. An SFX cue that masks the VO is a build defect, not a mixing taste
call: the final MIX is whisper-verified and any cue that degrades its line gets swept down.

**Sweep actually performed on build day (2026-08-03).** The first full render was whisper-verified
(medium) and word-diffed against the spine transcript: 285 vs 284 words, 5 differing runs, of which
exactly ONE was SFX masking - the 71.28 whoosh at 0.38 sat on the payoff word **"pop"** (71.36) and
the mix read *"they're just like"* with "pop" gone, while the spine alone reads *"it just like pop"*.
Swept offline (spine + cues summed at their real gains, `amix normalize=0`, re-whispered per step):
0.38 loses "pop", **0.20 restores it**; the riser came down 0.13 -> 0.10 in the same pass and was
re-verified. The 72.86 payoff IMPACT was left at 0.20 - the rule is to sweep the masking cue, never
the payoff hit. The other four diffs are NOT masking and were each proven so: 'plays' -> 'place'
(56.44) reads "place" even with that DING **fully muted**, so the ding is innocent; the dropped
stutter 'on' (8.84), an inserted 'because' (46.76) and 'as' -> 'is' (50.12) all sit in windows with
no cue playing.
