## Production conventions (this video)

### Line-tag legend (read this first; tags sit at the START of every line)

Each line does ONE job and is labeled. Tag = emoji + bracket, so it reads in raw text AND colors in the VS Code preview. Heavy `[NOTE]` / `[VERIFY]` content is pulled out into callout boxes. Canonical definition: `screenplay.md` Convention 5.

| Tag | Means |
|---|---|
| 👤 `[FACE]` | spoken, Mike's face on screen (gated, sparse, ONE sentence as punctuation) |
| 🗣️ `[COVER]` | spoken, voice over visuals (face off, the default) |
| 🔒 `[SAY-EXACT]` | spoken, the exact locked words (verbatim) |
| 🎬 `[SHOW]` | on-screen direction: b-roll, container, chart, receipt, transition |
| 💬 `[NOTE]` | a note / recommendation to Mike, NOT in the video |
| 🔍 `[VERIFY]` | confirm before it goes on screen |

- **Title-card flags (Convention 2 + the music-continuity rule):** a card lands ONLY at a chapter that STARTS A NEW music bed; a chapter that continues a bed flows in cardless, even if it teaches. The card set therefore FALLS OUT of the bed map in `## MUSIC-MOOD-PLAN`: **CH2** ("WHAT IS BITTENSOR?"), **CH4** ("THE CHALLENGERS"), **CH6** ("TOE TO TOE"). CH1 starts BED A but is a pure hook (card OFF); CH7 starts BED E but is the close (card OFF, the bed change itself is the break); CH3 and CH5 continue beds (OFF). On-screen card text is the short viewer-facing title, not the production name.
- **FACE / COVER (Convention 3):** gated face, OFF by default. `[FACE]` = ONE sentence as punctuation, then the next line is explicitly `[COVER]`. The vast majority of runtime is `[COVER]` over containers / b-roll / receipts. Every chapter gets a face beat or two (opener and/or payoff); mechanics chapters run almost entirely `[COVER]`; the CH7 CTA is the one sanctioned face-forward block.
- **Explainer visuals = system-design containers (Convention 4):** every teaching bullet gets a code-rendered HTML/SVG node-and-arrow diagram, spotlight-swapped one at a time. NOT tables, NOT AI images (labels and numbers must be pixel-accurate). Suggested role colors: subnets/miners = cyan, validators = indigo, TAO/money flows = amber, competitor lanes = neutral gray with their brand accent, warnings/attackers = red.
- **Charts guardrail (`skills/charts.md`):** a number WE control (the emission schedule, the conceptual heuristic curves) = our own code-built animated chart. Real market data (TAO / RENDER / VIRTUAL prices and market caps) = a real-site receipt on screen (taostats, CoinMarketCap, CoinGecko). NEVER an AI image as the source of any number.
