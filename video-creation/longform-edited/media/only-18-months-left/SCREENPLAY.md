## THE HOOK / THESIS

The first video showed the robots coming for the body: the cashier, the trucker, the trades, all the way up to the brain surgeon. This one is the part nobody wants to look at. AI software already came for the first wave of BRAIN jobs, the writers and the designers: fewer freelance posts (writing and coding posts down 21 percent, image posts down 17 percent), lower freelance pay, a US government forecast of zero growth for writers that names AI as the reason, and the first-ever World Economic Forum decline flag for graphic designers (DATA.md T3, Pillars 2 and 3). And the next wave has a date on it: in February of 2026 the CEO of Microsoft AI said most of the computer-desk TASKS of a lawyer, an accountant, a project manager or a marketer will be fully automated within 12 to 18 months (T1, CLIP-1). Mike's own call, said as his, is 18 months. Editors and actors are next (Mike's own edit is the inside receipt; Netflix, Particle6, H&M, Guess, Coca-Cola and China's first AI-made feature are the outside ones), universities lose both their hold on information and the jobs they prepare students for, and bookkeepers, accountants, paralegals and lawyers each get a sourced risk level. The paradox that makes it scary, Mike's own argument: wages disappear before prices collapse, so the people who make it through are the ones who already own assets (T5). The one hopeful turn: the last 30 days of medicine point at needing far less care. The close: own the assets AI makes more valuable, crypto among them for anyone with a phone, and find your people.
