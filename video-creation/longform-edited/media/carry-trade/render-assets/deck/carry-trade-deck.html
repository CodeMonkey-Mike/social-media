CH2 container — D-DUALFLOW

Two kinds of money, same trade

Lane 1 — Real money

Japanese savings

Life insurers, pension funds, banks → convert → foreign bonds (mostly US Treasuries) + foreign stocks, because Japanese assets paid ~0%.

GPIF: ~25% foreign equity

Lane 2 — Borrowed money

Leveraged speculators

Borrow yen at ~0% → convert → buy risk assets (stocks, crypto), a direct SHORT position on the yen.

The fast-unwind lane (CH3/CH4)

Both lanes only work while Japan pays ~0% AND the yen stays weak. VERIFY figures

CH4 container — D-WORKEDMATH

One trade, both sides moving against you

The loan

Borrow ¥ at ~0%

Yen strengthens 7% against the dollar.

Loan costs 7% more to repay

The asset

Hold the risk asset

Everyone else in the same trade sells at once to raise cash.

Asset price ALSO drops

Not two separate problems. One feedback loop, hitting from both sides at once. Illustrative generic numbers only, no invented specific trade (per the honesty box in SCREENPLAY.md).
