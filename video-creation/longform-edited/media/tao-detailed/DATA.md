**(c) The crises, in detail.**
- **2024 wallet drain (The Block, 2024-07-04, James Hunt):** attack began "7:06 p.m. UTC on July 2"; OTF detected an "abnormality in transfer volume" at 7:26 p.m. and put validators behind a firewall in "safe mode" at 7:41 p.m.; "The attack was traced back to the PyPi Package Manager version 6.12.2", "a malicious package was uploaded, compromising user security"; anyone who downloaded it "between May 22 and May 29" and then staked, delegated or transferred was likely affected; losses "$8 million" worth, "approximately 32,000" TAO; price fell "15%" to "around $230"; the protocol itself "uncompromised and secure" (https://www.theblock.co/post/303547/bittensor-exploit, read 2026-10-14). Safe mode lasted 2024-07-02 to 2024-07-12 (Bittensor docs wallets page, via search summary, not opened: [VERIFY the end date]). **Plain gloss for the strategist:** the chain was never broken; a fake copy of the official software library was planted in the public download store, and people who installed it had their keys read. Everyday analogy, if wanted: a counterfeit key-cutting kiosk, not a broken vault.
- **2026 Covenant AI exit (tao.media, 2026-05-14, Bart Hillerich):** on 2026-04-09 Covenant AI (Templar SN3, Basilica SN39, Grail SN81) announced it was leaving, saying "It is centralized control with decentralized branding."; founder Samuel Dare confirmed; Covenant "reportedly sold about 37,000 TAO worth of alpha tokens," about 11 million dollars; TAO "fell roughly 30%," about 900 million dollars of market cap; Templar's token fell more than 50 percent. ### Pillar 3: why it is a long-term play (the mechanics, sourced)
