## THE HOOK / THESIS

The AI that is coming for every job runs on compute, models and data that somebody owns, and Bittensor is an open network that pays the people who produce those things in TAO (DATA.md T1, bittensor.com/docs). So owning TAO, or staking it into a subnet, is a claim on that production instead of a wage from it (Mike's framing, said as his opinion; the deep dive on the idea retire-early opened in one chapter). The video earns the conclusion with detail: a dated backstory (a pseudonymous whitepaper, a 2021 halt and relaunch, a July 2024 wallet drain through a poisoned software package, a founder stepping down, a flagship team walking out, a government switching off a rival model for 18 days), the subnets doing the work (Lium's 1.3 million dollars of GPU billing in a month, Chutes' 160 billion tokens a day, a 72-billion-parameter model trained across the open internet on SN3), the mechanics that make it a long-term play (the 21 million cap, the December 2025 halving, 3,600 TAO a day, 64 percent staked, Root Reborn), the size of the prize (660 to 690 billion dollars of hyperscaler capex in 2026, as estimated), and the one caveat kept in CH4 (customer revenue is roughly a tenth of new issuance; Mike cut the caveats beat and the stacked synthesis at GATE 1, 2026-10-15, as too much FUD). CH1 opens ONE question (is TAO really the way to own a piece of the AI production, for the long haul) and CH5 answers it as Mike's conditional read, with a pass/fail watchlist. No price target, no promise, no "not having to worry" on screen.

| 💬 `[NOTE]` | a note / recommendation to Mike, NOT in the video |

| 🎞️ `[INSERT]` | a third-party clip plays with its OWN voice while the spine pauses (Convention 6). Names its CLIP-<n> row. Not spoken |

**Explainer visuals (Convention 4):** system-design containers, code-rendered, one per talking point, spotlight-swapped. Never tables, never AI images for text. No number on screen comes from an image model; real market and price data is a real-site receipt.

**Clip inserts (Convention 6):** CONSIDERED on every video. A third-party clip that plays with its OWN voice while the spine pauses is a 🎞️ `[INSERT]` line between its set-up line (which ENDS the sentence) and the resume line, plus a row in `## CLIP-INSERTS`; when nothing fits that section says NONE with the reason. Never a muted cover.

**Diction (persona):** TAO (never tau), Bittensor, Opentensor Foundation, Jacob Steeves, Ala Shaabana, Dynamic TAO (dTAO), subnet names as their teams write them (Lium, Chutes, Templar, Teutonic, Targon, Affine, Vanta, Ridges, Hippius, Macrocosmos, BitMind, OpenRoboto), Kaspa.
