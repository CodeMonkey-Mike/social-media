## The hook / thesis

This is a **title-bait satire**. The title and the first ~20 seconds are engineered to make the Kaspa
community angry enough to click and start typing, then the sarcasm leaks and they realize it is a love
letter to Kaspa wearing an XRP mask. Every "reason XRP is better" runs on one of two sarcastic inversions:

- **Inversion A: praise an XRP flaw as if it were a feature.** "Why WOULDN'T you want insider dilution,
  a monthly escrow dump, a four-year SEC lawsuit?"
- **Inversion B: dismiss a Kaspa strength as if it were a downside.** "Who would want a fair launch?
  Who wants proof of work? Who wants to actually mine the thing themselves? Sounds like homework."

The payoff: by the end the viewer has heard, in reverse, a complete and accurate case FOR Kaspa.

**Call to action mechanic (Mike's note):** the anger IS the distribution. Bait the click, bait the
comment, then flip it. The close invites the Kaspa community in on the joke and asks them to leave their
angriest-comment-turned-laugh below.

> Do NOT put a number on screen that has not been checked the day of the render. Tone is comedy but the
> facts still have to be real, or the joke breaks and the Kaspa crowd dunks back with a correction.

## Production conventions

**Title cards:** each reason gets an ON-screen card "REASON #N" with the deadpan sub-title (e.g.
"#1: Fair launches are overrated"). The card text is the JOKE stated flat, which is half the comedy.
Cold open + reveal = OFF (framing beats, flow straight in).

**[FACE] / [COVER]:** SPARSE face (house rule). Face is punctuation on the sincere-sounding punchlines,
the lines that sell the deadpan. The receipts (escrow schedule, premine chart, SWIFT/Linea headline,
court docket) all play [COVER] over a container or b-roll. Default is [COVER]; tag the one landing
sentence per reason [FACE].

**Comedy visual rule:** the receipt on screen should be REAL and play it straight, like a serious
explainer. The contrast between a sober chart and Mike's sarcastic praise is where the laugh lives. Build
receipts as code-rendered HTML/SVG containers (text accuracy), not AI images, per screenplay Convention 4.
