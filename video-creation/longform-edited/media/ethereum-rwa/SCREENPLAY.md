The ONLY allowed framing is the live asymmetry: massive real-world activity is choosing to settle on Ethereum's security, the value-accrual mechanics are still catching up, and that gap is the opportunity. ## Production conventions (this video)

### Line-tag legend (read this first; tags sit at the START of every line)

Each line does ONE job and is labeled. Tag = emoji + bracket, so it reads in raw text AND colors in the VS Code preview. Heavy `[NOTE]` / `[VERIFY]` content is pulled out into callout boxes. Canonical definition: `screenplay.md` Convention 5.

| Tag | Means |
|---|---|
| 👤 `[FACE]` | spoken, Mike's face on screen (gated, sparse, ONE sentence as punctuation) |
| 🗣️ `[COVER]` | spoken, voice over visuals (face off, the default) |
| 🔒 `[SAY-EXACT]` | spoken, the exact locked words (verbatim) |
| 🎬 `[SHOW]` | on-screen direction: b-roll, container, chart, receipt, transition |
| 💬 `[NOTE]` | a note / recommendation to Mike, NOT in the video |
| 🔍 `[VERIFY]` | confirm before it goes on screen |

- **Title-card flags (Convention 2 + the music-continuity rule):** a card lands ONLY at a chapter that STARTS A NEW music bed; a chapter that continues a bed flows in cardless, even if it teaches. The card set therefore FALLS OUT of the bed map in `## MUSIC-MOOD-PLAN`: **CH2** ("THE PROOF"), **CH3** ("ROBINHOOD CHAIN"), **CH4** ("THE BULL CASE"). CH1 starts BED A but is a pure hook (card OFF); CH5 continues BED D and flows in cardless (the close). On-screen card text is the short viewer-facing title, not the production name.
- **FACE / COVER (Convention 3):** gated face, OFF by default. `[FACE]` = ONE sentence as punctuation, then the next line is explicitly `[COVER]`. The vast majority of runtime is `[COVER]` over containers / charts / receipts / b-roll. Every chapter gets a face beat; the CH5 CTA is the one sanctioned face-forward block.
- **Explainer visuals = system-design containers (Convention 4):** every teaching bullet gets a code-rendered HTML/SVG node-and-arrow diagram or stat container, spotlight-swapped one at a time. NOT tables, NOT AI images (labels and numbers must be pixel-accurate). Role colors: institutions/issuers = indigo, Ethereum = its brand blue-violet, money flows = amber, **Robinhood Chain elements = Robinhood neon green (#CCFF00) or Robinhood yellow, NEVER greenish-cyan/teal** (that is Kaspa's signature color; persona `image_generation.robinhood_coin` + PROJECT-LOG carry-in), honest-gap/warning elements = red.
- **Charts guardrail (`skills/charts.md`):** a sourced number WE present (market growth, dominance share, lock-up percentages, the fee split) = our own code-built animated chart with the source tagged on screen. Live market pages (ETF flows, TVL trackers, article headlines) = real-site receipts. NEVER an AI image as the source of any number. AI generation is reserved for the text-free atmosphere/b-roll layer.
- **Diction (persona `spoken_voice`):** whole numbers as words in spoken lines ("thirty billion", "sixty percent"), exact digits on screen. **Exception (Mike, 2026-07-31): decimal numbers stay DIGITS even in spoken lines** — "5.7 million" and "0.15 percent", never "five point seven million" / "zero point one five percent". Spelling out the decimal point reads awkward on the page and is harder to voice naturally; digits are what an actor actually reads off a script. No unglossed jargon: tokenization, Layer 2, gas, staking, fee floor, and "money parked on the chain" (TVL) are all glossed inside their own beats below. Comma liberally.
