14 Nov 2025 to 14 Nov 2028, split by bucket. The build asserts every month's buckets sum to the tracker's own running total.

## How to animate (the method)

- Build every band's step polygon for all 36 months ONCE; reveal with a clipPath rect whose right edge moves by month
  (date-linear), never per-frame path rebuilding. The white total step line is clipped with them; a small white head dot
  may ride the top edge at the clip.
- Callouts / labels: fade + 8 px rise, 0.35 s. The marker line draws top-down (0.3 s) and its top dot pops.
- The switch-on: reveal x 653.33 -> 686.11 (0.35 s); the two slivers get a glow pulse (0 -> 1 -> 0.6, 0.6 s); legend rows
  Investors + Team & Advisors go from dim (.38) to lit (0.3 s).
