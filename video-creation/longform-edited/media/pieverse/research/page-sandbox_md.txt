## Safe uses
- Demonstrate response shapes in docs and OpenAPI examples.
- Help agents understand pricing, auth, and capability boundaries.
- Avoid creating, paying, or mutating production resources.

## Unsafe uses
- Do not use these values for production payments.
- Do not infer that write APIs or autonomous payment actions are public.
- Do not treat protected A2A or merchant SDK docs as reachable public resources.
