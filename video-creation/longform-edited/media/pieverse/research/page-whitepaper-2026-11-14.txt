Machine-to-machine payments require instant, frictionless settlement without human intervention.</li><li><strong>Auditability Deficits:</strong> On-chain confirmations exist, but lack structured, verifiable receipts for real-world applications like taxes, reimbursements, or legal compliance. This is critical as AI agents handle increasing transaction volume and need audit trails for accountability.</li><li><strong>Gas Barriers:</strong> Volatile fees and technical hurdles limit accessibility, particularly for micropayments and high-frequency AI-driven transactions. This creates significant friction that prevents autonomous agents from operating efficiently.</li><li><strong>Compliance Gap:</strong> As global regulators focus on crypto and AI agents handle increasing DeFi volume, the need for jurisdiction-specific compliance tools intensifies.
