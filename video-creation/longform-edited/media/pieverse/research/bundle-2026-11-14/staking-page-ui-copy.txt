FAQ AND PLAN COPY (in page order):
- Can I withdraw early?
- No. Every position stays locked until its selected term ends, and partial withdrawals are not available.
- Where do rewards come from?
- PIEVERSE rewards and XP are external Pieverse program benefits. They are not generated or guaranteed by the principal-custody staking contract.
- Will Human Staking earn XP?
- Yes. Each eligible daily boundary starts at 1 XP per staked PIEVERSE, then applies the selected term multiplier: 1x for 90 days, 1.2x for 180 days, or 1.5x for 365 days. XP is saved to your Pieverse account and can later be claimed to one Active Purr-Fect Claw.
- Why stake with a Purr-Fect Claw?
- Each eligible daily boundary starts at 1.1 XP per staked PIEVERSE, then applies the selected 1x, 1.2x, or 1.5x term multiplier. XP is credited directly to the selected Purr-Fect Claw Career after you confirm the position.
- Are token rewards or platform perks guaranteed?
- No. PIP-5 is an active community vote on possible token rewards, platform revenue, perks, governance, and other benefits. Amounts, eligibility, and claim rules are not finalized.
- Which networks are supported?
- Staking is available on Ethereum and BNB Chain.
- Principal locked until maturity
- No early or partial withdrawal
- XP accrues daily until maturity
