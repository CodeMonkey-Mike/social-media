  Understand the Staking Terms
    Pieverse Staking offers three fixed terms: 90, 180, and 365 days.
    Longer terms earn more XP per day. The duration of your one-time Agent benefit will also match the selected staking term.
    Please note:
    Your staked PIEVERSE will remain locked throughout the selected term.
    Early and partial withdrawals are not supported.
    Once the position reaches maturity, you must initiate the withdrawal manually.
    Every stake creates an independent position. Separate positions are not automatically combined.
    Before submitting a transaction, confirm the amount and term and make sure you are comfortable with the full lock-up period.
  Reward Rules
    Pieverse Staking offers two types of benefits:
    Daily XP
    A one-time Agent benefit
    1. How Is Daily XP Calculated?
    Daily XP is based on the amount staked, the staking method, and the selected term. The table below shows the combined multiplier for each option:
    Daily XP = Amount staked × Applicable combined multiplier
    The longer the staking term, the more XP you earn each day. For the same term, Purr-Fect Claw Staking always earns 10% more daily XP than Human Staking.

    When claiming the benefit, you can:
    Create a new Agent at the corresponding tier

  FAQ
  1. Do I need a Purr-Fect Claw to participate?
  No.
  You can choose Human Staking and complete the process directly from your wallet. If you already have a Purr-Fect Claw, you can choose Purr-Fect Claw Staking and earn 10% more daily XP than Human Staking for the same term.
  2. How much PIEVERSE can I stake?
  You can enter an amount based on your available balance. There are no fixed staking amount tiers.
  Please refer to the live Staking page for the minimum amount and supported decimal precision.
  3. What is the XP difference between the two staking methods?
  Both methods earn XP every day based on the amount staked and the selected term.
  For the same amount and term, Purr-Fect Claw Staking earns 10% more daily XP than Human Staking. See the Reward Rules above for the exact multipliers.
  4. How do I claim and use the one-time Agent benefit?
  Your staking amount determines the Agent tier, while the selected term determines the benefit duration:
  Less than 50 PIEVERSE: Starter Agent
  50 PIEVERSE or more: Basic Agent
  Each user can claim this benefit only once. Reaching maturity does not reset your eligibility.
  When claiming, you can create a new Agent at the corresponding tier or extend an existing Agent at the same tier.
  Before your first claim, multiple eligible positions can be combined to determine your Agent tier. For example, eligible positions of 20 and 30 PIEVERSE can be counted together as 50 PIEVERSE, qualifying for Basic. No Voucher Code is required to claim or use the benefit.
  5. Can I withdraw before the staking term ends?
  No.
  Early and partial withdrawals are not supported. Before confirming a position, make sure you are comfortable keeping the full amount locked for the selected term.
  6. How do I withdraw after my position reaches maturity?
  PIEVERSE is not returned to your wallet automatically when the staking term ends. You must initiate the withdrawal manually.
  To withdraw:
  Open the Staking page and connect the wallet used for the position
  Find the Position that has reached maturity
  Click Withdraw
  Confirm the transaction in your wallet
  Wait for the on-chain transaction to complete
  If the position is managed through Purr-Fect Claw, you can also ask the corresponding Agent to assist with the withdrawal and follow the prompts to confirm the transaction.
  A withdrawal may require a Gas Fee. Only positions that have reached maturity can be withdrawn.
  image.png
  7. Are multiple stakes combined into one position?
  No. Each stake creates an independent Position with its own amount, term, start time, and maturity date.
  The positions remain separate. However, before you claim the one-time Agent benefit, the amounts from multiple eligible positions can be combined to determine your Agent tier.
  8. How do I view and claim XP?
  XP accumulates daily based on the amount staked, the staking method, and the selected term. See the Reward Rules above for the exact multipliers.
  If you do not yet have a Purr-Fect Claw, XP earned through Human Staking will be stored under your account. Once you own and start using a Purr-Fect Claw, open the Agent’s Career XP page to view and claim the accumulated XP.
  XP earned through Purr-Fect Claw Staking is recorded under the corresponding Purr-Fect Claw. On the Agent’s Career XP page, you can view:
  Total XP
  Claimable XP
  XP History
  If claimable XP is available, follow the on-screen instructions to claim it.
  image.png
  XP reflects your long-term activity and growth with Purr-Fect Claw. Details about XP levels, benefits, and future utility will be announced through official Pieverse channels.
  9. What can XP be used for?
  XP currently reflects your long-term participation and growth with Purr-Fect Claw.
  Details about XP levels, benefits, and future utility will be announced by Pieverse. Any unconfirmed redemption or benefit information should not be treated as an official commitment.
  10. Do staking and withdrawals require Gas Fees?
  Yes.
  Token approvals, staking transactions, and withdrawals may all require on-chain transactions. You will need the selected network’s native token to cover the corresponding Gas Fees.
  11. How do I know whether my stake was successful?
  After the transaction is confirmed, refresh the Staking page or Agent page. If the new Position appears in the position list, the staking position has been created successfully.
  You can also check the transaction status in your wallet or on the relevant network’s block explorer.
