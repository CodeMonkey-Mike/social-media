## THE HOOK / THESIS

In December 2015, Product Hunt ran its first Golden Kitty Awards, and Robinhood won "Sexiest Product of the Year" and posted about it. On July 1, 2026, Robinhood launched its own chain, and on September 4, 2026 an independent fan token dedicated to that trophy, Golden Kitty (GOLDEN), launched on it. Its main pool trades against GLD, Robinhood's tokenized gold ETF token, so the token is priced in gold and the pool's fees are earned in gold. That puts it inside the stonk narrative: meme coins paired to tokenized stocks took off on Robinhood Chain in July 2026, the model spread to Solana and to crypto pairs, and on October 1, 2026 Solana's StonkFun added gold pairs, a month after Golden Kitty launched against gold. A brand-new chain with Robinhood's distribution behind it, a meme with a true origin story in Robinhood's own history, and gold on the other side of the main pool: that is the bullish case, and all of the upside stays conditional.
