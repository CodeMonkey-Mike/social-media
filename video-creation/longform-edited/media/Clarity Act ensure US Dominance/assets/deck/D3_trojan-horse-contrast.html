The Trojan Horse

Option A — the obvious way

Government CBDC

01Government builds its own digital coin: one ledger, total visibility.
02Citizens see a surveillance tool that watches every dollar.
03They reject it. Projects freeze in "pilot mode."
STUCK · going nowhere
Option B — what Washington did

Private stablecoin

01Let private companies build the dollar coin instead.
02People adopt it willingly: faster, global, it pays them.
03Regulated by Congress, enforced by the Treasury.
ADOPTED · by choice
Same control. None of the stigma. A quasi-private central bank digital currency people install themselves.
