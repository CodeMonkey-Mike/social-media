**Two-bill accuracy note (resolved — this is the real, current structure):** the reference video conflates two separate bills. This script keeps them straight, which makes it sharper and more current than the reference:

Coinbase makes ~$1.35B/yr on USDC rewards; the banks (ABA) are fighting to kill it.

## CH5 — The Fight Happening Right Now  (Title card: OFF, continues CH4's bed · register: gear 2)

> [!IMPORTANT] This chapter corrects the single most common misconception about this whole story (Mike had it, most YouTubers have it): people think the CLARITY Act bans interest on stablecoins. It doesn't. The ISSUER ban is the GENIUS Act (already law). And it only hits ISSUERS — not exchanges. That's why viewers are STILL earning yield on their stablecoins today: the platform (Coinbase) pays it, and the ban never reached the platform. The CLARITY Act is where the fight to CLOSE that loophole is now playing out. Nailing issuer-vs-platform is the payoff of the video.
> Verified facts: GENIUS Act (law, July 2025) prohibits payment-stablecoin ISSUERS from paying yield. Coinbase pays ~3.5% APY on USDC balances, calls it a "loyalty reward," funded by a revenue-share of Circle's reserve interest — an AFFILIATE, not the issuer, so the ban doesn't reach it ("the Coinbase-shaped hole"). ~$1.35B/yr to Coinbase. OCC + the banks (ABA/BPI) want the loophole closed; the CLARITY Act (House-passed July 2025, STALLED in Senate) is the current battleground. 🔍 re-confirm the APY + Coinbase figure + Senate status at record.

**Beat 1 — the ban is real, but it's not the bill you think, and it's not who you think**
- 🗣️ [COVER] Here's the part that trips everyone up, and honestly it tripped me up too. There is a real interest ban. But it's not in the CLARITY Act, and it does not do what people think it does.

- 👤 [FACE] But here's the thing. You are probably still earning yield on your stablecoins right now. So what gives?

**Beat 2 — the Coinbase-shaped hole**
- 🗣️ [COVER] The ban stops at the issuer. It never touched the exchanges. Circle can't pay you. But Coinbase can hand you about three and a half percent and call it a loyalty reward, because Coinbase isn't the issuer.

- 🗣️ [COVER] Same money, same interest, it just takes one extra hop so it doesn't count as the issuer paying you. The law has a Coinbase-shaped hole in it.

**Beat 3 — the fight, and why it decides dominance**
