## THE HOOK / THESIS

The President of the United States told TIME, in his own words, that "Certain levels of inflation will also pay off that debt very rapidly" (TIME transcript, interview Sept 28, 2026, published Oct 1). In plain English, inflation pays a debt by making each dollar worth less, so a 40 trillion dollar debt shrinks because your dollars do (the mechanism is documented: Reinhart and Sbrancia, BIS Working Paper 363). America has done it before: after World War II (long rates capped at 2.5 percent while inflation hit 19.7 percent in March 1947; debt held by the public fell from 106.3 percent of GDP in 1946 to 22.2 percent in 1974) and again in 2020 to 2022 (rates at zero for 2 years, inflation 9.1 percent in June 2022). Doing it on purpose makes people angry, so Mike's SCENARIO, framed as "here is how it could go" and never as fact, is that a crisis makes the printing look like a rescue. And when money is printed into a crisis, the owners of scarce assets come out richer: 2020 to 2021 brought a record 2,755 billionaires and, by 2022, a record 19 crypto billionaires (Forbes). Mike's opinion and bet, first person, never a promise: crypto billionaires will be made next year. Will you be one of them?
