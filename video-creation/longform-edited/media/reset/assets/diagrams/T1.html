The playbook

This has been done before.

1942 to 1951
After World War II
Long-term rates capped at 2.5%
Then inflation hit 19.7% (March 1947)
2020 to 2022
After the pandemic
Rates at zero for two years
Then inflation hit 9.1% (June 2022)
Sources: Federal Reserve History, The Treasury-Fed Accord · BLS CPI-U · FOMC statements, Mar 15, 2020 and Mar 16, 2022
