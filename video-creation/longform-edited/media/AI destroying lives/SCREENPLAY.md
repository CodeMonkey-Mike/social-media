## THE HOOK / THESIS

This is not a future threat. Real people are already out of work and have been searching for months: 2.0 million Americans were long-term unemployed in October 2026 (BLS), and some of the people in the opening montage say in their own words that AI took their work. The countable damage sits in three places: layoffs where the employer itself named AI (121,401 in 2026 through October, Challenger), the bottom rung (entry-level postings down 7.5 percent while senior postings are up 14.7 percent, Indeed), and freelance language work (translation demand down 30 percent in the first year after ChatGPT). For office work the evidence is the government's own forecast: minus 752,100 jobs by 2035, with AI named as a reason. And the trades people are sure no robot can do are being approached from two sides: ordinary robots already weld in production, and a humanoid welder is a prototype that is learning, with field tests planned for 2027. The video shows exactly where each one stands, and never further.

The others describe a long search; two of the news pieces carry an expert saying AI is not the main cause. Airable instead: the locked CH1 line, "Some of those people say AI took their work."

> [!WARNING]
