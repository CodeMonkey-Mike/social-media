## Round 5 (what changed after visual-qa failed round 4, 2026-11-24)

- **The sieve read as painted over the coins.** It was painted under them: the faded coins were see-through
  (opacity 0.26), so the mesh and the rim showed through. A faded coin is now a solid washed-out coin that hides what is
  behind it. Every coin on a sieve stands with its foot on the back half of the disc, on a small contact shadow, and is
  painted after all three sieves.
- **Every coin on a sieve is inboard.** Pile on sieve 1: nine on the mesh, the row ends 27 px inside the rim tips (ten
  before, the ends on the tips). Sieve 2: five in a row, 51 px inside. Sieve 3: four in a row, 21 px inside (five before,
  the end coin on the tip). The coin turned away is an inner one on every sieve. That is why three coins come out,
  not four, and why the pour is 18 coins, not 24: every timing above is re-derived.
- **A coin that was flagged and then turned away keeps a limp grey flag**, so it does not read as one of the coins the
  bots never flagged.
