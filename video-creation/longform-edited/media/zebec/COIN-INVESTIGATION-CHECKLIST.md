# How To Investigate ANY Coin — reusable framework

_Seeded 2026-07-11 during the ZBCN video. Mike asked to save the coin-evaluation points from the reference
videos so future coin videos can reuse the method. This is the durable checklist; each video applies it to
one coin. Distilled from three creators (Uncle Ray / Crypto Bellwether's "3 Rabbit Holes," a bitcoins.com
on-chain teardown, and a "Top 5" panel) plus the ZBCN investigation itself. Add to it every time a new
angle proves useful._

> **Core discipline:** separate **the business** from **the token**, and separate **what you can verify**
> from **what they ask you to trust**. A great business can wear a dead token. Marketing numbers,
> self-reported metrics, and partnership tweets are claims until an independent source confirms them.

---

## 1. Tokenomics & supply structure (weight this heaviest)
- Circulating vs total vs max supply. **Emission/inflation schedule** — is supply still expanding, and for
  how long? (tokenomist.ai, CoinGecko, the project's own tokenomics page.)
- **Unlock cliffs:** how much insider / VC / foundation / team supply is still locked, and when does it
  hit the market? Locked supply = future sell pressure the current chart cannot see.
- **Fully diluted or not.** "Fully diluted, no one left to dump" is a real bull thesis ONLY once the
  unlocks are actually done. Confirm the date; do not take it on faith while emissions are still running.
- **Value accrual:** does the token actually capture the protocol's success (fees, revenue share, real
  buyback/burn), or is it a governance token with no economic claim on anything?
- **Burn mechanism:** real and revenue-funded, or cosmetic? A burn only matters if the protocol earns real
  money. And ask: **can you verify the burn on-chain**, or are you trusting a press release?

## 2. Adoption / utility / real revenue
- Actual usage: users, clients, transaction/payroll/TVL/revenue numbers.
- **Cross-check every self-reported number against an independent source.** Do the project's Twitter, its
  homepage, and on-chain data agree? Inconsistent self-reported metrics (e.g. "11K employees / $48M" in one
  place, "50K users / $1B" in another) is itself a red flag.
- Is the product actually shipping (roadmap milestones hit), or is it a promise?

## 3. On-chain / liquidity health (the part marketing can't spin)
- **Liquidity depth vs market cap.** A thin on-chain pool against a large market cap (e.g. a ~$449K pool
  behind a ~$369M cap) means the "market cap" is fragile and CEX-driven, not real on-chain demand.
- **Holder distribution + whale vs retail flow.** Are large wallets accumulating or distributing? If
  whales are exiting while retail count balloons late, the skilled money is leaving. Copy the whales.
- **Developer activity:** GitHub commit history. A dead repo for months on a "live product" is a warning.
- **Presence where real projects show up:** DefiLlama, DexScreener, a working app (does it actually load?).

## 4. Competition
- Web2 AND Web3 incumbents. Can the project realistically displace the giants (e.g. ADP + Microsoft in
  payroll), or does it have to tie INTO them? Is it "a league of its own," or one of many?

## 5. Market structure & derivatives
- Where does volume live: on-chain vs CEX (spot vs perps)?
- **Funding rates + open interest.** Deeply negative funding + falling OI can mean a rally was a **short
  squeeze**, not real demand; once funding normalizes the squeeze is over and price deflates.
- How much leverage is in the system? (Most alt volume is leveraged perps; big money sells into pumps.)

## 6. Cycle history & probability
- **Has it made a NEW all-time high in a LATER cycle?** ~94-95% of alts never make a new ATH after their
  first cycle. Distinguish "a new cycle high" from a true new ATH.
- Where is price vs the prior-cycle ATH and prior-cycle low? That sets realistic target math (e.g. "5x
  just to reclaim the old high; 16-20x only if it retests the low then makes a NEW high").
- **The bear-market-darling trap:** coins that pump DURING a bear (because their story is hot when nothing
  else works) can **top early** in the next cycle and then round-trip down while holders wait for more.
  (A panelist's example of the pattern.)

## 7. Narrative / hype / FOMO
- Strength of the story + attention. Short term, "FOMO and hype trump everything."
- **Attention decoupling from price is a warning** (lots of views/engagement, price still bleeding).
- Treat big partnership announcements as potential **sell-the-news** events, not automatic pumps.

## 8. Macro / Bitcoin regime
- Bull vs bear regime; alts follow BTC. Bad-tokenomics alts bleed in a bear no matter how good the story.

## 9. Team
- Who is behind it, their track record, and how they got their supply (fair launch vs VC allocation).
- **Verify the current leadership** (founder vs current CEO can differ; titles change).

## 10. Position management (evaluation's twin — plan the exit before the entry)
- Scale out: recover your initial stake early, take "handfuls" into pumps, optionally keep a small
  moonbag to round-trip the next cycle.
- Keep dry powder; wait for a level to hold or a retest rather than chasing.
- Be willing to flip short after a top (most of the market is leveraged; pumps rarely sustain).
