## 1. What Zebec actually IS (the product)

- Founded **2021** by **Sam Thapaliya** (Nepalese founder, b. 1998; CS from Menlo College; 4 US patents;
  earlier co-founded an AI/analytics startup, ASMI). `[S7]`
- Started as a **"money streaming" protocol on Solana** — pay salaries/vesting by the second instead of in
  bulk on payday. `[S6][S7]`
- Has since **pivoted / expanded into "PayFi"**: real crypto **payroll**, the **Zebec Card** (spend
  anywhere Mastercard is accepted; Silver / Carbon / Black tiers), treasury tools, and a **SuperApp** that
  bundles payroll + card + treasury + staking + rewards. `[S1][S9][S10]`
- **SuperApp** cleared regulatory review and was **submitted to the Apple App Store + Google Play in Q2
  2026.** `[S1]`
- VC backing: raised **$28M+** from **Circle, Solana Ventures, and Coinbase Ventures.** `[S7]`

## 2. THE GOOD (bull case)

**Real, measurable usage (this is the strongest part of the story) — Nov 2025 figures:** `[S9]`
- **~$47M monthly payroll volume** ( → **$500M+ annualized** ).
- **~12,600 employees paid** across **~239 enterprise clients.**
- **Zebec Card: ~$60M annualized transaction volume**, virtually issued in **97 countries**, "thousands of
  clients."
- Payroll expanding **beyond crypto/tech into schools and healthcare.** `[S9]`
- **~106K token holders** (Jun 2026). `[S8]`

**Blue-chip validation:**
- **Nacha Payments Innovation Alliance member** (joined Dec 19 2025, announced Q1 2026 as 1 of 20 new
  members) — Nacha governs the **$85T US ACH network**; fellow members include **JP Morgan, Wells Fargo,
  Fiserv, Circle, ADP.** `[S11]` **(See the caveat in §3 — this is a working-group membership, NOT a
  "governing seat.")**
- **USD1 stablecoin integration** — USD1 is issued by **World Liberty Financial (the Trump-family DeFi
  venture)**. Live in the Zebec SuperApp; Zebec says it now runs **USD1 payroll for 65,000+ workers**. A
  real distribution + narrative + political-tailwind hook. `[S12]`

**Tokenomics turned a corner (the deflation pitch):**
- **Final token unlock completed March 2026** → no more scheduled dilution/unlock selling pressure. `[S1][S5]`
- Shifted to a **deflationary buyback-and-burn** funded by **product revenue** (payroll fees, card fees).
  Zebec says **buyback volume is growing ~70% annualized.** `[S5][S9]`
- **Staking (mid-2026):** ZBCN staked in the SuperApp unlocks tiered rewards on **Zebec Card spending** →
  ties holding the token to real utility + pulls supply off the market. `[S1]`
- Exchange access widening: **Indodax** (Indonesia's largest, Jul 9 2026), **Fasset** (125+ countries). `[S3][S9]`

## 3. THE BAD (bear case)

**Price / history — the two-token timeline (state precisely, see the age/drawdown boxes in §0):**
- **Company** founded 2021. **ZBC** (original token): IDO/TGE **March 15-16 2022** on Raydium/Solana at
  **$0.021**; peaked **$0.05273 (Apr 2022)**; bled for two years. `[S6][S15]`
- **ZBCN** (current token): **migration 1 ZBC → 10 ZBCN, trading started April 10 2024** `[S15]`. This is
  what CMC's "Zebec **Network**" chart shows, so the chart only goes back to **~April 2024 (~2 years)**.
- **Drawdown, done right:** ~**-69%** from the ZBCN-era high ($0.0071, May 2025) `[S3]`; ~**-58%**
  split-adjusted from the 2022 ZBC peak; ~**flat** vs the 2022 launch price. **NOT -98%** (see §0 box).
- So the honest point is NOT "down 98% and dead." It is: the **company has had ~4 years and a full cycle**
  to prove the token, the **token itself has traded ~2 years**, and it is **still a sub-cent micro-cap**.
- **~100B supply → optically "cheap" ($0.002)**, which is a psychological trap: a move to even $0.02
  requires a **~$2B** market cap (roughly 10x). Big supply means the price *looks* like it can "easily
  10x" while the market cap says otherwise. `[S3]`

**Serial rebrands / narrative-chasing:**
- **ZBC → ZBCN migration (1:10) in 2024** (10B ZBC → 100B ZBCN, holders got 10 ZBCN per ZBC; value
  unchanged, just re-denominated). `[S1][S6]` Second identity, second token.
- An independent explainer's blunt read: ZBC spent long stretches **"moving without a clear narrative,"**
  the original streaming-payment product **"never attracted a significant user base,"** it **relies on
  Solana**, and it **"lacks distinct advantages in product functionality."** `[S6]` The 2026 PayFi/payroll
  story is the *latest* narrative, not the first.

**Marketing runs ahead of the facts (the credibility tax):**
- The Nacha line Zebec pushes ("a seat in the body that governs the $85T ACH network, alongside JP
  Morgan") is **inflated**. The Payments Innovation Alliance is a **membership/working group** that added
  **20 members in a single quarter** `[S11]`; it is not a governing board seat and it does not make Zebec a
  peer of JP Morgan. The partnership is real; the framing is puffed.
- **Burn amounts are NOT publicly verifiable on-chain** `[S5]` — the deflation pitch is **trust-me**, not
  **verify-me**. It only works if revenue keeps funding buybacks, and we can't independently audit the burn.

**Structural / token-value-accrual doubt:**
- It is **VC-funded (Circle / Coinbase / Solana Ventures), NOT a fair launch** `[S7]` — the exact opposite
  of the fair-launch thesis Mike applies to Kaspa. Early insiders/VCs have a cost basis the market doesn't.
- **Does product success actually reach the token?** The ONLY hard link from "Zebec the business" to "ZBCN
  the token" is the buyback, and buyback volume, while growing, is small relative to a ~$213M cap and 100B
  supply. A payroll company can thrive while its token stays flat.
- **Skeptics exist:** the token has drawn public "rug pull" accusations in crypto-creator circles (e.g. a
  TikTok creator calling Zebec a rug) `[S13]`; in 2023 Zebec **reacquired ~300M ZBC from investors and
  burned tokens**, which some read as opaque `[S13]`. Nothing here is a proven fraud, but the trust
  question is live and should be represented honestly, NOT dismissed and NOT amplified into an accusation.
- **Political entanglement cuts both ways:** the USD1 / World Liberty (Trump-family) tie is a distribution
  and narrative tailwind AND a regulatory / reputational risk concentrated in one politically charged
  counterparty. `[S12]`
- **Solana-dependent** for its core rails. `[S6]`

## 4. The real question the video should land (framework)

Separate **the business** from **the token**. The business is real and measurable. The token is a
speculative claim whose only mechanical link to the business is a small, unverifiable buyback. The bet is:
*will revenue scale fast enough that buyback + staking demand overwhelm a 100B float before the market's
patience (already 4 years thin) runs out?* Keep any upside **conditional** ("if the flywheel spins"),
never a promised target. This is a "real product, speculative token, eyes-open asymmetry" verdict, not a
shill and not a hit piece.
