## 3. How to animate (the 16:9 method, portrait numbers)

- Build every mark from `geom.points` (SVG `<text>` at fixed coordinates, never re-measured per frame).
- **Links draw** with a clip rect whose right edge runs from `a.x` to `b.x` (works for the dashed link).
- **Floor lands:** the bar scales x 0 -> 1 from its centre (0.2 s), then the wash + chevron fade in and rise 8 px (0.3 s).
- **Dot lands:** scale 0 -> 1.15 -> 1 (0.3 s) with the r 24 halo.
- **Capsule lands:** grows outward from its centre to x 463.1-561.8 (0.4 s).
- **Band fills** left to right across x 96 -> 246.1, then its right edge + dashed top.
- **Callouts** fade in + rise 8 px (0.35 s) on the landing beat.
- **Rest vs lit:** REST = links, marks, callouts at .55 and the panel title muted #505a6e; LIT = full and the title in the
  series colour (GOOGLE cyan #00c2ff, ANTHROPIC green #00e68a).
- **Entry is OPACITY ONLY** (`assets/vertical/NOTES.md` item 1): 0.35 s fade, NO scale on the wrapper (the 16:9 spec's
  0.97 -> 1 scale-in locked the SVG text at the wrong layout per render session). Verify per NOTES item 2 after the render.
