## 3. How to animate (the 16:9 method, portrait numbers)

- Bars grow from the zero line (height 0 -> 280 down / 0 -> 400 up, ease out cubic); the dashed slot footprint fades out as
  its bar starts.
- Values count by whole points with the bar (0 -> -7, 0 -> +10), landing exactly on the cue word; the sign stays fixed.
- The +10% bar's glow fades in over its last 0.2 s.
- The BLS sentence types on character by character, the `BLS` tag first, then line 1, then line 2: **the two lines are fixed
  strings at fixed positions** (no reflow while typing).
- The arrow draws along its path (path length via `getTotalLength` once, then strokeDashoffset), the head pops at the end.
- **Entry is OPACITY ONLY** (`assets/vertical/NOTES.md` item 1): 0.35 s fade, NO scale on the wrapper. Verify per NOTES item 2.
