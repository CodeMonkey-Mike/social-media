## The design (why two panels)

Two labelled series, never merged: **GOOGLE** (cyan `#00c2ff`, "share of NEW code that is AI-generated and approved by
engineers") on the left, **ANTHROPIC** (green `#00e68a`, the stylesheet's lit accent, the payoff series) on the right, on ONE
time axis (2024-10-01 to 2026-10-01, x linear in UTC date) and ONE 0-100% scale, so the two slopes compare directly. One plot
was tried on paper and rejected: Google's 75% (Apr 2026) and Anthropic's line into "more than 80%" (May 2026) land within 6 px
of each other, so Anthropic's line would run straight through Google's 75% dot and read as a shared point.

Mark grammar (the legend under the panels prints it): **dot** = a stated figure (75%) · **floor** = a bar at the value with an
open top (chevron + upward wash) for "more than" / "over" (more than 25%, more than 80%, over 90%) · **capsule** = a season,
not a day ("fall 2025", Pichai's own look back, drawn Sep 1 to Nov 30 2025) · **band** = "low single digits", 0 to 5% from
the axis start to Feb 2025 (no number printed) · **dashed link** = two different measures (merged code -> codebase
contributions). Straight links join two statements; the legend says nothing between them is reported.

Plot box (panel-local px): x 90 -> 780 = 2024-10-01 -> 2026-10-01 (0.94521 px/day); y 620 = 0% -> 170 = 100% (4.5 px/pt).

## How to animate (the method)

- Build every mark from `geom.points` (never re-measure text per frame: SVG `<text>` at fixed coordinates, no jitter).
- **Links draw** with a clip rect whose right edge runs from `a.x` to `b.x` (works for the dashed link too; no dashoffset).
- **Floor lands:** the bar scales x 0 -> 1 from its centre (0.2 s), then the wash + chevron fade in and rise 8 px (0.3 s).
- **Dot lands:** scale 0 -> 1.15 -> 1 (0.3 s) with the r 22 halo.
- **Capsule lands:** grows outward from its centre to x 406.6-491.7 (0.4 s).
- **Band fills** left to right across x 90 -> 219.5 (gradient wash, then its right edge + dashed top).
- **Callouts** fade in + rise 8 px (0.35 s) on the landing beat.
- **Rest vs lit:** a series at REST = its links, marks and callouts at opacity .55 and its panel title muted `#505a6e`; LIT =
  full opacity and the title in the series colour.
