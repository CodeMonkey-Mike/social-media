## THE HOOK / THESIS

AI now writes most of the new code at the companies that build AI, by their own dated statements: 75% of all new code at Google is AI-generated and approved by engineers (Sundar Pichai, 2026-04-22, DATA T1), and over 90% of the contributions to Anthropic's codebase in September 2026 were written by Claude (Anthropic S-1, filed 2026-10-26, DATA T2). Both companies describe the same change in the engineer's day: direct and review, not type (DATA T3). So code got cheap, and the engineer gets paid for the business outcome the code moves. One engineer directing AI agents can now cover most seats of the old product pod (DATA T6, T7, conditional), which makes that engineer responsible for a piece of the business: that is what "business-minded and entrepreneurial" means in practice. Four versions of the engineer each own a different piece, mapped on the AAARRR funnel: growth engineer, product engineer, platform engineer, GTM engineer (DATA pillar 3). The market data says the demand did not vanish, it moved toward people who direct the work and own a result (Indeed, BLS, DATA pillar 4); the engineers who refuse the change are the ones who fall behind.

> [!WARNING]
> **DO NOT AIR "75% of Google's code is written by AI" (all of Google's code, or with no human).** Pichai's sentence covers NEW code, "AI-generated and approved by engineers". Airable: "75 percent of all new code at Google is generated by AI, and engineers approve it" (2026-04-22). The locked hook carries both halves; do not drop "new" or "approve".

> [!WARNING]
> **DO NOT AIR "we've reached recursive self-improvement" or "the AIs can improve themselves now"** (the brief's own wording). Anthropic: "We are not there yet, and recursive self-improvement is not inevitable." Its threshold call is about EXPECTED future models, not current ones (S-1/A No.2, 2026-11-10). Airable: "the loop has started closing": each version of Claude helps build the next, and on 2026-11-10 Anthropic told investors it has met its own threshold for accelerated AI progress, "based on what we expect from future models" (accuracy pass 2026-11-13: the 2026-11-02 "may imminently meet" and "not designated" sentences were REMOVED in No.2; never air them).

> [!WARNING]
> **DO NOT AIR "AI writes 100% of the code at Anthropic", "Amodei was right, 90% of all code is written by AI", or ANY percentage for the whole industry or the whole world.** The 100% remarks were not opened at a primary; Amodei's 2025-03-10 line was a forecast; the sourced 90% is Anthropic's OWN codebase, September 2026. Airable: the two primary Anthropic figures ("more than 80%" of merged code, May 2026; "over 90% of the contributions", September 2026), always with company and date.

> [!WARNING]
> **DO NOT AIR "engineers at Anthropic are 45 times (or 8 times) more productive", or "Microsoft is at 30%, Meta at 50%, OpenAI at 80%" as current facts.** The 45x is daily VOLUME of code, not productivity, and the essay calls its own 8x an overstatement; the Microsoft figure is from April 2025 and the others were never opened at a primary. This script uses none of them.

> [!WARNING]
> **DO NOT AIR "software engineering jobs are disappearing" or "nobody is hiring developers", and do not use the stale "27.5% (or 30%, 35%) below pre-pandemic".** Indeed, 2026-11-12: software development postings UP 23.8% year over year, 19.2% below February 2020. Airable: "the demand didn't disappear, it moved": 71% of the increase went to senior roles, entry level is 4.5% of postings.

> [!WARNING]
> **DO NOT AIR "software developers aged 22 to 25 are down nearly 20%", "AI cut young developers' jobs by 19%", or "it's easier for young people to get in now".** The 19% (Stanford, August 2026) is a GAP against less-exposed peers across ALL highly AI-exposed occupations, descriptive, not causal; entry-level postings are down. Airable: "19 percent behind their peers in less exposed jobs, mostly because fewer are getting hired", and the E6 form: the next generation arrives with the mindset, into fewer junior openings.

> [!WARNING]
> **DO NOT AIR "forward-deployed engineer postings up 800%", "3,000 GTM engineer jobs" or "GTM postings up 205%", "Spotify squads were 6 to 8 people" or "Amazon's two-pizza team", a year or venue for the pirate funnel ("2007, Ignite Seattle"), or "Clay invented the GTM engineer" as fact.** None was opened at a primary. Airable: "typically 10 people or fewer" (Scrum Guide 2020) · "Dave McClure's Startup Metrics for Pirates" with no date · "Clay, the company that says it coined the title in 2023".
