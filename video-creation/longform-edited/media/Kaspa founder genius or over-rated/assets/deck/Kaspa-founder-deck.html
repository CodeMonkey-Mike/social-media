GHOST · 2013

Greedy Heaviest Observed Sub-Tree

Speed Bitcoin up and blocks get orphaned faster than the network can agree, and throwing them away weakens security. GHOST counts the orphaned work too, then picks the heaviest sub-tree.

main chain (selected) orphaned blocks, their work still counted

Ethereum's whitepaper cites this paper. Early Ethereum shipped a simplified variant, the uncle reward. (Not the full fork-choice.)

SPECTRE · 2016

The names get weird. You can forget this one.

Serialization of Proof-of-work Events,

The one line that matters: a cryptocurrency with no blockchain at all. Blocks made in parallel, votes decide the order. A research blueprint, not a coin you can buy.

From Chain to BlockDAG

A single-file chain becomes a web

Bitcoin blocks (one line) SPECTRE blocks (a web / DAG) fast pairwise votes, but no single total order yet

SPECTRE nailed the head-to-head (did A come before B), but not the full lineup. That gap sets up GHOSTDAG.

PHANTOM / GHOSTDAG

Keep every block, and still agree on the order

GHOSTDAG keeps every honestly created block, uses how well connected each one is to pick the honest cluster, then lays them all down in one agreed order. This is the consensus Kaspa runs.
