### [FACE] / [COVER] markers (when is Mike's face on screen?)

The spine is a **gated face** (house rule #6): Mike's face is NOT on screen by default. It appears only on
the beats tagged **`[FACE]`**; every other beat is a full-screen **`[COVER]`** (b-roll or a container),
audio always playing under it. Same convention as the silverscript / banks-own-chain scripts.

- **`[FACE]`** = viewer sees Mike, full-screen, direct to camera. Punctuation on the beats that carry
  conviction or connect with the viewer.
- **`[COVER]`** = VO keeps going while the screen shows a container / diagram / b-roll / image. This is
  the DEFAULT, so an untagged beat is a cover.
- **`[FACE] HOLD`** = deliberately stay on the face, no cutaway.
- **Tag at the BEAT (bullet) level, on the bold label** — you do NOT need the exact scripted words; the
  gating is a per-beat call. For beats written out as spoken lines (the `>` blocks) you MAY tag per
  sentence instead (finer control), like the cold open + the CH8 asymmetry beat.
- **Rule of thumb:** if a beat TEACHES a concept, it's probably `[COVER]`; if it's Mike making a point
  or connecting with the viewer, it's `[FACE]`. Every chapter has at least one `[FACE]` beat (usually its
  opener and its payoff). The mid-roll plug (CH7) is `[FACE]` throughout; CH5 mechanics are almost all
  `[COVER]` (the system diagrams).
- **KEEP FACE SPARSE (Mike's rule, 2026-06-16):** face moments are individual sentences here and there,
  used as PUNCTUATION. NOT entire blocks of sentences, and NOT every-other-sentence. The vast majority of
  the video is `[COVER]`; `[FACE]` is the rare cut back to his eyes that makes a single line land. When a
  beat runs several sentences, put `[FACE]` on the ONE sentence that hits, cover the rest.
- **A `[FACE]` cut = ONE sentence (Mike's rule, 2026-06-16).** Show his face for a single sentence only;
  more than one sentence ONLY if they are very small/tight (e.g. "Gone. For everyone."). After the face
  sentence, **tag the very next line `[COVER]` explicitly** so the face never visually carries across the
  lines that follow it. Untagged still = COVER, but inside a `>` verbatim block (cold open, asymmetry beat)
  write the `[COVER]` return out so the gating is unambiguous, not inferred.
- All tags below are proposed defaults, flip any beat.
