## What it is
Code restatement of Stanford's two reads as a GAP: a full bone bar = where young-worker employment would be at the pace of less-exposed jobs (same age); a red bar under it = where it is in the most AI-exposed jobs. The hatched gap widens 15% -> 19% while the big counter counts 15 -> 19 and the chip row hands off from the July 2025 read to the June 2026 read.
