## 2. Pillar facts

### Pillar 1: What Kaspa is

- **Proof of work, fair launch, no premine, no pre-sale.** kaspa.org home: "No premine. No hidden allocation. Fair launch." and "Bitcoin block hashes embedded in the genesis coinbase serve as timestamps, ruling out hidden premining." (https://kaspa.org/, read 2026-11-03). kaspa.org/lore, Brief history: "Mainnet launched in November 2021 as a fair-launched proof-of-work network." "No premine, insider allocation, or pre-sales." "Every coin in circulation has been mined in the open from genesis onward." (https://kaspa.org/lore, read 2026-11-03).
- **Mainnet launch date: 2021-11-07.** Community wiki: "28,704,026,601 KAS in the period of 36 years from the mainnet start (November 07, 2021)" and "The pre-deflationary phase, that started at the mainnet start, Nov 07 2021, has already ended (at May 08, 2022)." Also: "Was there any kind of premine? Nah. Fair launch model, a Nakamoto's philosophy." and "Was there any type of ICO, vesting phase or something like this? No. Only a publicly declared fair launch." (https://wiki.kaspa.org/en/tokenomics, read 2026-11-03; community wiki, corroborated by kaspa.org/lore "November 2021").
- **blockDAG, a fully implemented GhostDAG (persona wording), parallel blocks instead of orphans.** kaspa.org page title: "Proof-of-Work blockDAG for Real-Time Decentralization"; /lore: "Real-time decentralization is Kaspa's north star." and "Kaspa is a live proof-of-work blockDAG running at 10 blocks per second." (read 2026-11-03). The protocol paper: "PHANTOM and GHOSTDAG: A Scalable Generalization of Nakamoto Consensus", Yonatan Sompolinsky, Shai Wyborski, Aviv Zohar, received 2018-01-30 (https://eprint.iacr.org/2018/104, read 2026-11-03). Plain-language summary the sources support (for the strategist, not a quote): Bitcoin throws away blocks found at the same time (orphans); Kaspa keeps them all in a directed acyclic graph and GhostDAG orders them, which is what lets the network run 10 blocks a second on proof of work.
- **Blocks per second today: 10.** "Kaspa is a live proof-of-work blockDAG running at 10 blocks per second." (https://kaspa.org/lore, read 2026-11-03). Crescendo (KIP-14 "The Crescendo Hardfork", status Active in the KIP README, https://github.com/kaspanet/kips, read 2026-11-03) moved the network from 1 to 10 BPS; rusty-kaspa v1.0.0 notes: "Crescendo is scheduled to activate on mainnet at DAA Score 110,165,000, projected to occur on May 5, 2025, at approximately 15:00 UTC." (v1.0.0 notes as mirrored at https://newreleases.io/project/github/kaspanet/rusty-kaspa/release/v1.0.0, read 2026-11-03; the GitHub releases list was opened at https://github.com/kaspanet/rusty-kaspa/releases, read 2026-11-03, and confirms v1.x then v2.0.0).
- **Emission schedule (the "chromatic halving").** Community wiki: "The pre-deflationary phase, that started at the mainnet start, Nov 07 2021, has already ended (at May 08, 2022). Reward rate was firstly random, in a range of 1 to 1000 Kas per block, for the first 2+ weeks, and then was replaced with the constant one at the 1st hardfork: 500 Kas per second." "The chromatic phase started after that. In this phase block rewards geometrically decrease. The initial block reward was 440 KAS. The block reward halves once per year, but smoothly: every month, block reward is decreased by a factor of (1/2)^(1/12)". (https://wiki.kaspa.org/en/tokenomics, read 2026-11-03). The code: rusty-kaspa `consensus/src/processes/coinbase.rs`: "These values represent the reward per second for each month (= reward per block for 1 BPS)"; table starts `44000000000, 41530469757, 39199543598, ...` (sompi), index 12 is `22000000000`; per-block subsidy is the per-second value divided by BPS ("SUBSIDY_BY_MONTH_TABLE[i].div_ceil(bps)"), so a block-rate change does not change the emission rate (https://raw.githubusercontent.com/kaspanet/rusty-kaspa/master/consensus/src/processes/coinbase.rs, read 2026-11-03). 41530469757 / 44000000000 = 0.9439 = 2^(-1/12), computed here.
