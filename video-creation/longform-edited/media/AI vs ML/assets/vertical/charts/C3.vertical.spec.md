**Count-up rules (unchanged):** one decimal while counting for bars 1 and 2 and the gap; integers for bar 3; the final
frame shows the exact DATA string. No overshoot or bounce on a bar or a number: the height IS the value. Every text is
SVG `<text>` at fixed coordinates (no per-frame re-measuring = no jitter); the plate is a fixed rect from the JSON.

**Guards held (unchanged wording):** prints 155.5K not 155,000 and 339.5K, never 350K; never TYPICAL ENGINEER alone;
the bracket is labelled TOP 10% vs MEDIAN, never median vs median (W1, W2); the junior-skew caveat rides on line 2 of
the source line for the whole hold (DATA T6). No em dashes on screen.

**Edge safety:** every text element sits at least 44 px inside the frame (gate in `_shot_portrait.py`): the 105% camera
wobble crops 27 px a side and 48 px top and bottom.
