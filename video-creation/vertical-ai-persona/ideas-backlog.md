# Ideas Backlog — vertical AI-persona crypto videos

_Created 2026-05-30 after the first video ("When did your husband stop touching you?" — clinic→resort
testimonial parody for CryptoRich.vip) shipped to all 7 platforms._

## The idea engine (how to generate more of these)

The first video worked because it was **format parody**, not "a crypto joke." It took a recognizable
real-world format (weight-loss / relationship testimonial infomercial) and swapped the subject to crypto.
That collision IS the comedy. So every concept = **familiar format × a crypto truth everyone recognizes.**

- Don't search for "crypto jokes" — they come back generic. Brainstorm the collision instead.
- Keep Mike's real likeness as the through-line (interviewer / pitchman / patient / expert, etc.).
- Tone: sex appeal + humor, deadpan parody of ad culture + crypto-bro culture (per persona).
- Most should soft-land on **CryptoRich.vip** (end card + the YT/Rumble/BitChute description link),
  but not every one needs a hard plug — some can just be funny and build the persona.

### Research notes (2026-05, what's trending — informs freshness, not the core joke)
- **AI street/vox-pop interviews (Veo3-style) are the #1 viral format right now** — the clinic-interview
  format is squarely in this lane. Keep mining it.
- Validated crypto pains: panic-sell at the worst moment, 3am chart-watching, liquidations, rug
  pulls/honeypots, FOMO/FUD, "financial advice = goodbye to your liquidity," telling the spouse, selling
  too early, "down 90% still bullish."
- Current narratives for topical spice: PIPPIN rug, political/"brand" tokens, AI-themed coins, meme-coin
  ETFs, "fairer launches," honeypot contracts, the supercycle/"high-beta" talk.
- SNL-proven ad formats: pharma-ad-with-fast-disclaimer, robotic testimonials, earnest infomercial pitch.
- New native format: "nervous system" text-overlay (imagined catastrophe vs. reality).
- Length: 7–15s hooks travel hardest; 30–90s for the story pieces (our testimonial was ~76s and that's fine).

## How to use this
- Pick a concept, write the storyboard (scenes with `turns[]`), then run the proven pipeline.
- Prioritize **A (interviews)** and **B (pharma)** first — interviews match the trending format AND our
  validated recipe; pharma disclaimers are reliably funny and easy to script.
- Keep it evolving: add concepts as they come, mark ones we've made, note which hooks performed once we
  have analytics (the dashboard tracks views per platform).
