# CTA voice script — longform-to-short outro

The one spoken line that closes every short condensed out of this project's longform.
Canonical text lives HERE; `tts-chunks.json` is derived from it and gated against it by
`video-creation/skills/higgsfield-voice/verify-tts.js` before any credits are spent.

Markup per `higgsfield-voice/SKILL.md`: ALL-CAPS only on content words of 5+ letters ("WATCH"
qualifies). Voice preset: MIKE-CLONE.

⛔ **NO trailing `!` on this line.** `!` lifts the END of the line, so the stress landed on "video"
(measured: 1.86-2.30s, the longest-held word in the take, near peak energy, rising finish). Mike heard
it immediately: "it seems like video is emphasized." A period lets the line fall and leaves the CAPS on
WATCH as the only emphasis. Reserve `!` for lines whose LAST word is the one that should punch.

**Chunk 1 — CTA**
> Click below to WATCH the full video.

🎬 SHOW: full-frame outro card, "WATCH THE FULL VIDEO"
