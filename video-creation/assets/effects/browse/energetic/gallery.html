Energetictheme id kinetic-text-2023 · 17 devices · 10 with an engine

a head-to-head or straight-to-the-point explainer picks this theme whole: the signature open on the hook, pops on the verdict words, bands for the 4 s statements, the list card for enumerations, headline pops over b-roll, the subscribe close. Longer house videos may pick it for ONE chapter. The 007 additions widen the same theme: the running caption line (kt-caption-highlight) on the face lines, the typewriter card for a summary / definition, the stacked list over b-roll for an enumeration that rides on footage, the statement over b-roll for a 4 to 6 s sentence, the text wall for the ONE biggest idea of the video, the sparkle on a caption's key word, and 0.1 s push-slide cuts between cards and b-roll where 004 used whip-zooms. A video picks per line; one theme, two source dialects.

Players: demo = the engine's reference render from _demo/ (approved by Mike), source = the excerpt of the source video at the device's cue, both with sound.
