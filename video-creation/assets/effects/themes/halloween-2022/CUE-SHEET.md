## 7. Pattern to reuse

- Horror register, dark, fast: a keyed or seated face, word pops in flat green, every b-roll change hidden under a split
  (transitions library), b-roll cut-aways with short dissolves, a camera shake held on the face after a montage or a
  cut-away, and ONE big montage per act (the zoom slideshow for a chapter opener, the blood promo for the act break).
- In the source almost nothing fires a sound: the music (Harvester, Wicked Cinema, catalogued `harvester`) carries it.
  The only device sound is `woosh9` under the subscribe button. The pumpkin stinger brings its own baked sound.
- The screenplay may pair a catalog sound with a silent device (written on the `[SHOW]` line); the engines' sound helpers
  take it as `pair` and land its attack ON the event frame.
