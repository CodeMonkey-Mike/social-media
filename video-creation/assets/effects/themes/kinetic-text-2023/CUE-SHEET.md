## Pattern to reuse
- Every text, label, icon and card lands WITH a sound; never silent. One sound per event, never two stacked.
- Two role labels = two different sounds, fixed for the whole video (A = cash-register, B = wooden-rattle-whoosh).
- Keyword pop on the face = knock-pop. List items = ting, one per item. Scattered words = pop-1. Big red word = punch-1-2023.
- Cuts and whip-pans = whoosh-4 by default; fast-reverse-1/2/5 for variety; swoosh-8 for a chapter-level whip.
- Music bed under all of it (the 2023 bed was Bobo Renthlei, Story Of My Life, instrumental), ducked, never stopped for a pop.

## Pattern to reuse (what 007 adds to the 004 list)
- The caption line IS the caption on every face shot; a phrase that is the headline pops instead (lime + white, two lines).
- Cards and footage push in and out in 3 frames (left in, right out; top in, down out); the whip-zoom stays for the chapter-level cut.
- A list over footage: headline on a rattle, items on fast-reverse-5, one per spoken word; a summary gets the typewriter card instead.
- One text wall per video, on the biggest idea, with the red punch word on a cash register.
- The subscribe bar rises onto the chest and clicks itself; the playlist line gets the mouse click.
- The bed never stops: -20 dB under the open, -25 dB under the rest.
