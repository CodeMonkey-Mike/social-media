## Pattern to reuse
- Every text, label, icon and card lands WITH a sound; never silent. One sound per event, never two stacked.
- Two role labels = two different sounds, fixed for the whole video (A = cash-register, B = wooden-rattle-whoosh).
- Keyword pop on the face = knock-pop. List items = ting, one per item. Scattered words = pop-1. Big red word = punch-1-2023.
- Cuts and whip-pans = whoosh-4 by default; fast-reverse-1/2/5 for variety; swoosh-8 for a chapter-level whip.
- Music bed under all of it (the 2023 bed was Bobo Renthlei, Story Of My Life, instrumental), ducked, never stopped for a pop.
