## Key Patterns

- **Face bottom is the default** (3/4 videos), content zone on top
- **Screen share content vs b-roll split varies widely** — Video 1 is 100% b-roll; Videos 3 & 4 are mostly screen share with heavy overlays; Video 2 is ~80/20 b-roll/screen share
- **CoinMarketCap** is the most common screen share content — used in 3/4 videos
- **Meme/celebrity inserts** appear in every video (Michael Rosen, Saylor, Trump, The Rock, GTA character, Pepe, Stonks man, Vince McMahon, "NO WAY" shocked man)
- **Full-screen b-roll** used sparingly — ~1–2× per video for ~2–3s each, typically as an opening intro or single dramatic beat
- **Full-screen face** is rare — used only for closings or punchy moments
- **Overlays are extremely high-frequency** — Video 3 alone has 17 overlay events in 50s (~1 every 3s). The 0.8fps scan captured only 8 of them
- **Coin-specific branded overlays** — each new coin gets a thematic visual: ELIZA.OS blue banner, ACT I anime eye, AI16Z orange logo intro
- **Emotion/reaction system** — green sparkle+up arrow for pumping, red sparkle+down arrow for dumping. Meme clips sync to content (Stonks = "pumped to a billion", "NO WAY" = can't get in early)
- **Color-coded captions** — white=standard, RED=shocking numbers, GREEN=bullish numbers
- **Screen zoom + cursor scrubbing** — editor zooms into the CMC chart and drags the cursor along the timeline to highlight historical prices. Appears 2× in Video 3
- **Layout switching** — the split-screen → full face cam → full b-roll → split-screen transitions are deliberate pacing tools, not random
- B-roll/overlay cuts at roughly **1 new event every 2–3 seconds** in the most produced videos — never static for long
