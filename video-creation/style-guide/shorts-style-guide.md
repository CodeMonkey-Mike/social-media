# Shorts Style Guide
_Built from frame-by-frame analysis of 4 crypto short-form videos_

## Layout

- **Split-screen is the default:** face-cam in one zone, dynamic b-roll in another
- Hard horizontal dividing line (box or band) separates the two zones
- Face zone: ~40% of frame, usually top or bottom depending on creator preference
- B-roll zone: fills the remaining ~60%
- Full-screen breaks used 1–3 times per video for maximum-impact moments

## Captions

- **2–4 words per caption** — word-by-word or short phrase pacing
- **Position:** Middle band between face and b-roll zones — never overlapping either
- **Style A (dark theme creators):** Bold white text, black outline/stroke, mixed case
- **Style B (same creator both AI videos):** Yellow highlight color for emphasis words, white otherwise
- Never more than one short phrase on screen at once
- Timing: roughly one caption per hard cut

See [captions.md](captions.md) for text style and animation specs (shared across all formats).

## B-Roll

Changes every **1–3 seconds**. Never static. Sources used:
- CoinMarketCap / chart screen recordings (credibility signal)
- AI-generated art (Pixar 3D, anime, cinematic renders)
- Meme images (Stonks guy, The Rock, reaction faces, GTA character)
- Animated logo/coin graphics
- Abstract motion (glow flashes, fade-to-black, glitch)
- Stick figures / 2D vector animations
- Real news article / tweet screenshots

## Hook Structure (first 3 seconds)

Never open with "so today I want to talk about." Always one of:
1. **Name drop + contradiction:** "I listened to Michael Saylor... he says crypto more than bitcoin now"
2. **Number first:** "AI16z dropped 300 million market cap"
3. **Cinematic title card:** Full-screen anime art + bold title text before dropping into talking head
4. **Outcome first:** "If this does a two-billion market cap, my community does a 2000x"

## Accent Colors

Each creator picks ONE accent color and uses it consistently throughout:
- Teal/cyan = Kaspa brand color (used in logo, bars, captions, b-roll tint)
- Yellow = urgency/CTA color (used in captions and "BUY NOW" / "Link in Description" graphics)
- Grey = competing asset (Bitcoin shown grey vs. teal Kaspa)

## Transitions

- **Hard cuts: 95%** of all transitions
- Flash/glow (yellow or white): used once per video for a dramatic beat
- Fade-to-black: used for pause/emphasis moments, not as a generic transition
- No dissolves, wipes, or zoom transitions

## CTA Pattern

Near the end:
- Explicit "Link in Description" text graphic with a red down-arrow
- Verbal CTA matches the graphic
- "Buy now" / "get in early" language
- Always the last 5–8 seconds

## Energy / Tone

- Insider-knowledge voice: "everybody knows," "you can't get better than this"
- Urgency: numbers, percentages, market caps stated rapidly
- Casual speech patterns preserved in captions (contractions, slang)
- Creator on camera at all times — b-roll never replaces the face entirely for more than ~3 seconds

## What This Means for Mike's Videos

- Face-cam should be in a fixed zone (top or bottom), consistent across all shorts
- Every talking point needs a b-roll cut — no static face-cam for more than 2–3 seconds
- Captions go in the middle band, not over the face or over the b-roll
- Lead with the number or the outcome — Kaspa price target, market cap projection, or the contradiction (Saylor said X)
- Pick one accent color tied to the content (teal for Kaspa content)
- Hard cuts only — don't waste time on fancy transitions
