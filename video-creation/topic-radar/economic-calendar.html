QE / liquidity watch

Treasury buybacks

  • Aug 19: Treasury doubled long-end liquidity buybacks from $2B to at least $4B per operation, effective Sep 9 and running through Nov 4.
  • This week has the last two scheduled ops: Wed Nov 4 (10Y to 20Y, at least $4B) and Thu Nov 5 (1M to 2Y, $4B). No TIPS or cash-management ops scheduled.
  • The story: the Nov 4 refunding decides whether the bigger buybacks continue, grow, or get cut back. Expanding = more stealth-QE talk; shrinking = Treasury thinks the long end is fine.

Fed bill purchases (Reserve Management Purchases)

  • The Fed started RMPs (buying T-bills to keep reserves "ample") after the Dec 2025 FOMC; ~$250B of bills bought from January to mid-2026, $10B to $40B a month.
  • RMPs are currently PAUSED. NY Fed schedule for Oct 15 to Nov 13: about $14.6B reinvestment purchases, $0 RMPs. Zero since mid-August (last: ~$10B in the Jul 14 to Aug 13 window).
  • The Oct 28 directive still lets the Desk "increase SOMA holdings through purchases of Treasury bills" when appropriate, and reinvests agency paydowns into bills.
  • The story: the Fed held rates with two officials wanting a HIKE, and Fed bill buying is paused. The next schedule (~Nov 13) says whether the liquidity tap turns back on.
