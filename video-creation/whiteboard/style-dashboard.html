Whiteboard style picker

Two separate decisions. A. Drawing vocabulary: HOW things are drawn (bodies, faces, objects), 7 candidates on one identical scene. B. Pen: ink, wobble, fills and fonts, 14 candidates, each shown on a simple scene and on richer drawings. Every candidate stays as an option (a multi-chapter video may want a dark pen in one chapter and a light one in another); the badge marks the current pick. Star, leave notes, Export, paste the JSON to Claude. Frames: video-creation/whiteboard/samples/

A. Drawing vocabularies

Same scene every time: a person, a horse, a vault, three coins, a tree. Only the way of drawing changes.

Hand-drawn with hair stick-haircurrent pick

Round heads, hair variants, tapered shirts, stick limbs, an illustrated horse with mane and hooves, ground shadows. Wobbly ink, flat pastel fills.

Bean characters beanMike, 2026-10-30: too playful

No necks: tall pill bodies with the face on the body, stubby arms and legs, a plump horse with tufted mane and tail. Clean thin outline, rounded everything.

Geometric flat geo-flatMike, 2026-10-30: does not want the other vocabularies

No outlines, no faces: circle heads, rounded-rectangle torsos, thick rounded limbs; the horse built from rounded blocks. Two tones per object, corporate-flat.

Pictogram glyphs pictogramMike, 2026-10-30: more serious, but bland

Everything a solid one-color silhouette like airport signage; one accent color marks the thing that matters. Zero texture, maximum legibility at small size.

Isometric blocks isometricMike, 2026-10-30: too playful

Every object is a three-tone cuboid on an isometric grid: a boxy horse, a cube vault, cylinder coins, a low-poly tree. Reads as a tiny 3D diorama without any 3D package.

Pixel art pixelMike, 2026-10-30: too playful

Sprites on a 22 px cell grid, hard edges, a small fixed palette, bitmap heading. Instantly recognizable and cheap to author as text maps.

Pure line art line-onlyMike, 2026-10-30: does not want the other vocabularies

Ink outlines only, no fills anywhere, a single accent color for emphasis. Elegant and fast to read; everything stays an outline.
