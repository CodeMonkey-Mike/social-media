What they actually are

Two Different Bets on AI

Layer 1 blockchain · mainnet 2020

NEAR Protocol

  • Started as NEAR.ai in 2017, an AI company that pivoted to blockchain in 2018
  • Co-founder Illia Polosukhin co-wrote "Attention Is All You Need", the Transformer paper behind ChatGPT
  • NEAR AI Cloud: private AI inference inside secure hardware
  • NEAR Intents: one-click swaps across chains, built for users and AI agents
AI runs on the chain.
128 subnets · "A Peer-to-Peer Intelligence Market"

Bittensor (TAO)

  • Every subnet is a competition that produces one AI commodity
  • Compute: Lium (SN51) rents out GPUs
  • Inference: Chutes (SN64) serves open-source models through an API
  • Training: Covenant-72B, a 72B-parameter model trained across the open internet on Templar (SN3)
AI is the product.
NEAR right now

The Vote and the Fund

HSP-029 on House of Stake · closes Nov 2

The Supply Vote

2.5% → 1.6%
new NEAR a year, phased in over 24 months
  • Quorum hit on day 2: 2.23M veNEAR voted, 1.85M needed
  • 21 for, 1 against: the lone no is 94 veNEAR (67% yes needed)
  • All the voting power is about 0.4% of all NEAR
  • Then validators decide: in 2025 they passed a cut the vote missed
A small group sets the supply for everyone.
Bitwise NEAR ETF (NRR) · trading since Sep 29

The Fund

+1.1M then -257K
NEAR in one session on Oct 19, then its first outflow
  • The +1.1M was 12 days of new supply (about 90,000 NEAR is issued a day)
  • Now holds 17.35M NEAR after 50,000 shares were redeemed
  • Stakes 100% of it, so the cut lowers its own yield
  • Holds 3x+ the voting power of House of Stake, but isn't voting
The biggest buyer doesn't vote on its own supply.

TAO has no vote like this. Its supply is set by code: a 21M cap and Bitcoin-style halvings.

Vote as of October 23, 2026; fund holdings as of October 22, 2026. Sources: House of Stake (HSP-029), nrretf.com.
