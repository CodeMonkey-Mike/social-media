Chapter 02 — 1990s

The Cypherpunk Movement

Who Were They?
  • Activists, mathematicians & engineers
  • Believed in privacy & freedom through cryptography
  • The Cypherpunk Mailing List — where the ideas were born
  • Core belief — governments & corporations can't be trusted with financial data
Key Figures
Timothy C. May
Author of the Crypto Anarchist Manifesto (1988)
Eric Hughes
Author of A Cypherpunk's Manifesto (1993)
David Chaum
Pioneer of cryptographic digital cash — DigiCash (1989)

Chapter 03 — Pre-Bitcoin

Failed Attempts at Digital Money

💸
DigiCash — 1989
David Chaum's early digital cash — went bankrupt 1998
🪙
e-gold — 1996
Gold-backed digital currency — shut down by US government 2008
🔩
Bit Gold — 1998
Nick Szabo's proposal — never implemented, but a direct blueprint for Bitcoin
💾
b-money — 1998
Wei Dai's proposal — also never implemented
The lesson — every attempt was either centralized or failed to solve the double-spend problem

Chapter 04 — 2008 / 2009

Satoshi Nakamoto & The Bitcoin Whitepaper

October 31, 2008
Bitcoin whitepaper published — "Bitcoin: A Peer-to-Peer Electronic Cash System"
Who is Satoshi?
Anonymous — identity never confirmed to this day
January 3, 2009
The Genesis Block is mined — Bitcoin's first ever block
Embedded in the Genesis Block
"Chancellor on brink of second bailout for banks" — not an accident
January 12, 2009
First ever Bitcoin transaction — Satoshi sends 10 BTC to Hal Finney
2010
Satoshi disappears — hands off development and is never heard from again

Chapter 05 — 2009 to 2012

The Early Days of Bitcoin

2009
Bitcoin launches with zero monetary value
May 22, 2010 — Bitcoin Pizza Day
10,000 BTC paid for two pizzas — the first real-world Bitcoin transaction
2010
Mt. Gox launches — Bitcoin's first major exchange
February 2011
Bitcoin hits $1 for the first time
2011 – 2012
Developers, libertarians & early adopters begin to build the ecosystem
Those 10,000 BTC would be worth hundreds of millions of dollars today

Chapter 09 — 2022 to Today

The Crypto Winter & Beyond

The Collapse of 2022
  • Terra/LUNA — May 2022, $40 billion wiped out overnight
  • Three Arrows Capital & Celsius — contagion spreads
  • FTX collapse — Sam Bankman-Fried, the biggest fraud in crypto history
  • Bitcoin drops below $16,000
The Recovery
  • Bitcoin ETFs approved — US, January 2024
  • Bitcoin hits new all-time highs — 2024
Industry Standard Definitions
❄️ Crypto Winter
Price at or below the 200-Week SMA
🐻 Bear Market
Price below the 50-Week SMA and/or the 200-Day SMA
These moving averages are the benchmarks traders use to define market conditions
