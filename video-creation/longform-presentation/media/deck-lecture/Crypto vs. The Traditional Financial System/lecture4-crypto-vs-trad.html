Control

Who's Really in Control?

Traditional Finance
Centralized
  • Bank can freeze your account
  • Transactions can be reversed
  • More money can be printed
  • Access can be denied
  • Rules can change overnight
Crypto
Decentralized
  • No one can freeze your wallet
  • Transactions are irreversible
  • Supply is fixed by code
  • Open to anyone, anywhere
  • Rules enforced by math

In the traditional system, you don't own your money. You own a claim on money held by someone else.

Visibility

Transparency vs. Opacity

Traditional Finance
Behind Closed Doors
  • Bank ledgers are private
  • Fed balance sheet updated weekly
  • Audit trails controlled internally
  • You trust the institution's report
  • Fraud can go undetected for years
Crypto
Public by Default
  • Every transaction on-chain forever
  • Anyone can verify any balance
  • Supply provably fixed by code
  • No trust required, just math
  • "Don't trust, verify"

The blockchain is the first financial ledger in history that is open to everyone and controlled by no one.

Honest Assessment

The Tradeoffs

Volatility
  • · Not a stable store of value (yet)
  • · Price swings can be extreme
No Chargebacks
  • · Transactions are irreversible
  • · Mistakes cannot be undone
No Insurance
  • · No FDIC equivalent for crypto
  • · Exchange failure = potential total loss
User Responsibility
  • · Lose your keys, lose your crypto
  • · No one to call for help

These tradeoffs are real, and understanding them is what separates informed investors from gamblers.

The Big Picture

Not Either/Or

Bitcoin ETFs
Approved by the SEC in January 2024. Wall Street now sells Bitcoin exposure through traditional brokerage accounts
Stablecoins
Dollar-pegged crypto assets with the speed and openness of crypto with the stability of fiat, now used by hundreds of millions
CBDCs
Central Bank Digital Currencies. Governments worldwide building digital versions of their currencies on blockchain rails

The future isn't crypto vs. traditional finance. It is crypto becoming part of traditional finance. That shift is already underway.
