Slide 1 · EVAA Just Went 11x in Three Weeks, And 39x From My Call
    • From the June 6 low of $0.28 it is about 20x. The all-time high of $13.62 came 24 days after launch, then it fell about 98% to that low.
    • CoinMarketCap shows a market cap of $104M, not $38.6M (it counts more tokens as circulating, slide 5). If a viewer quotes $104M, that is why.

Slide 2 · A Pump With No News
    • The only "news" sites point to: a FameEX perp listing on November 16, after the move had already started. KuCoin spot listed it October 5, three weeks before the first leg.
    • The 3-to-1 short figure counts accounts, not dollars, and only on Gate. Say "more traders betting against it", not "a short squeeze" as a fact.
    • Funding stayed mild (about 0.01% to 0.06% per 8 hours on Gate). Binance, Bybit and KuCoin futures could not be read from here, so the real totals are larger.

Slide 3 · A Lending App Inside Telegram
    • TON's coin now shows as GRAM on CoinGecko ("Gram, prev. Toncoin"). Same coin.
    • One pre-seed angel is described in EVAA's own docs as someone who "owned a network of Telegram channels with more than 20 million subscribers".
    • Right now the app's best advertised rates: USDT in the Stable Pool 17.4%, GRAM in the LP Pool 16.6% (EVAA Telegram, November 14). They change daily.

Slide 4 · The Token Went Up. The Deposits Went Down.
    • The $118M on the home page is "deposits at the peak" and likely counts borrowed money too; DefiLlama's peak is $84.3M. Do not try to reconcile them on air.
    • No fee or revenue number exists. Upper bound if you need one: $3.6M borrowed at 10% interest is about $360K a year, and that goes to lenders before any protocol cut.

Slide 5 · How Many Tokens Are Out?
    • The 17.9M figure comes straight from EVAA's own server (api.evaa.space); CoinMarketCap marks it "self-reported".
    • About 0.95M of the 50M cap is not minted yet; the TON side can still mint it.

Slide 6 · What Holding EVAA Gets You Today
    • EVAA's active channel is a second one, @evaatg (16.8K subscribers), posting every two or three days. Views per post halved since late September (about 4.5K to about 2K).
    • X (@evaaprotocol) was not checked. If someone says "they posted on X", that may be true.

Slide 7 · Priced Like a Lending App With 141x the Deposits
    • On CoinMarketCap's market cap ($104M) EVAA is about 16x its deposits, not 5.8x.
    • EVAA's 24h volume ($42M) is about 47x Venus's.
