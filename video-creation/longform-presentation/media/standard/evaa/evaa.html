What EVAA is

A Lending App Inside Telegram

"The first decentralized lending protocol on TON"

How It Works

  • Lenders deposit GRAM (TON's coin), stablecoins or staked TON and earn interest
  • Borrowers post collateral worth more than the loan
  • Liquidators step in when a loan's collateral runs thin
  • Runs as a Telegram Mini App, plus a web app and a payment card
Started at a TON hackathon, 2023

Who's Behind It

  • CEO Vlad Kamyshov, co-founder Aleksandr Sudeikin
  • Raised $2.5M in a private token sale, January 2025
  • Backers include TON Ventures, Animoca, Polymorphic Capital, CMT Digital
  • Audited by Quantstamp and Trail of Bits. No exploit on record

Token launched October 3, 2025 on Binance Alpha and Binance Futures. It lives on TON and BNB Chain, and 61% of the supply sits on BNB Chain.
