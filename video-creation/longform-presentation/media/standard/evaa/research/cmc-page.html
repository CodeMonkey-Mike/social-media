About EVAA Protocol

What is EVAA?

EVAA is a liquidity protocol integrated with Telegram designed to make yield, payments, and borrowing simple. It provides a DeFi superlayer on Telegram, allowing users to earn yield, make payments, and borrow directly via the Telegram Mini App — @EvaaAppBot — or its web app. Users can easily earn income on their assets, get over-collateralized loans, and in the near future, make seamless card payments and get non-collateralized loans.

Who Are the Founders of EVAA?

The EVAA executive team consists of four core members with extensive experience in finance, development, and marketing. Vlad (CEO) has 3 years in decentralized finance, including successful experience building two lending protocols, and 4 years in risk management in traditional finance. Vladislav (CTO), formerly of TON Foundation, is a 7-year development veteran with 4 years in DeFi application development. Alexander (CGO) has 5 years of experience as a founder and was previously a game developer. Alexander (CMO) has 7 years of experience in crypto product marketing and co-founded a Telegram mini-app accelerator.

What Makes EVAA Unique?

EVAA stands out as the first and leading lending protocol in Telegram. The project has achieved over $30M in total deposits ($118M at its peak), engaged over 310,000 unique wallets, and processed over $1.4B in transaction volume. It is backed by prominent investors like Animoca Ventures, TON Ventures, and Polymorphic Capital, and has been audited by Trail of Bits and Quantstamp, ensuring a high level of security and credibility.

What is the EVAA Token ($EVAA)?

$EVAA is a deflationary utility token with a precisely calculated release schedule to prevent downward price pressure and contribute to stable growth. The $EVAA token has a wide range of use cases within EVAA Protocol. These use cases for $EVAA have been designed to promote growth and composability across the broader TON DeFi ecosystem. They are part of the structured journey toward achieving decentralization, community governance, and protocol autonomy. Token holders can participate in the governance of the EVAA Protocol by voting on selected platform-level technical proposals and feature updates.

How Many EVAA Tokens Are There?

The token distribution is allocated across various segments to foster ecosystem growth. Key allocations include 22% for airdrop and liquidity provider rewards, 20.08% for the DAO treasury, 16.5% for founders and team, and 17.22% for pre-seed and seed investors. The initial circulating supply at the Token Generation Event (TGE) will be 13.38%, with only a 1.71% unlock, designed to reduce immediate selling pressure.

Where Can You Use EVAA?

EVAA is accessible directly within the Telegram ecosystem through its verified Mini App, @EvaaAppBot, and via its web application at app.evaa.finance. The protocol is also integrated with all main ecosystem players, including the official Telegram @Wallet, TON Space, TON Keeper, MyTonWallet, OKX Wallet, Binance Wallet, and Bitget Wallet, ensuring broad accessibility for users."
