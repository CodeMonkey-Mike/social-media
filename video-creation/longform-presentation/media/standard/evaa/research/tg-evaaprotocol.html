EVAA is becoming a Community-Owned DAO 😇

You’ve been waiting to take part in the protocol’s key decisions — and we’re making that happen very soon.


The latest article on the TON Blog highlights our current achievements, future vision, and even reveals a few upcoming features we haven’t talked much about yet…

📝 Did you know that…


EVAA’s journey began at the 2023 Hack-a-TON x DoraHacks event, where co-founder Alexander Sudeykin introduced one of TON’s first-ever lending applications.

The goal was to bring DeFi to Telegram users 💎


💳 Card Payments: Users will be able to spend crypto through traditional payment networks directly inside Telegram, bridging DeFi yields with real-world purchases.

🏦 Undercollateralized Loans: By leveraging Telegram’s social infrastructure and reputation systems, EVAA aims to reduce collateral requirements, making borrowing more capital-efficient.

The community is the heart of EVAA — and this next chapter will give you more power, more tools, and more ways to shape the future of DeFi on TON together 🤑
