## Utility & Governance & Rewards

The $EVAA token has a wide range of use cases within EVAA Protocol. These use cases for $EVAA have been designed to promote growth and composability across the broader TON DeFi ecosystem. They are part of the structured journey toward achieving decentralization, community governance, and vital protocol autonomy.

### | Governance:

Token holders can participate in the governance of the EVAA Protocol by voting on selected platform-level technical proposals and feature updates.

This decentralized governance process ensures that certain elements of the platform’s development and future direction are driven by its active users.&#x20;

**Platform Governance proposals might include, in the future:**

• Assist in deciding when, for how long, and with what limits to engage in selected buybacks and burns of $EVAA tokens on the secondary market, to assist in stabilising volatility

• Admission, setup or suspension of token lending pools

• New Proposals for tokens collateral & selection of isolated market candidates.

• Proposals for token incentives on selected elements.

• Specific platform updates.

• Platform Risk-management and platform fees policies

The $EVAA Token is not intended to convey and it will never grant any form of governance or voting right in any company or entity directly or indirectly connected with the EVAA Protocol and software platform.

### | Utility:

**Utilities include:**

• Fees reduction: Users who burn $EVAA tokens in selected ways will benefit from a discounted protocol/platform fee as opposed to regular EVAA Protocol users;

• $EVAA may be used for staking or locking within “security modules” to guarantee the platform’s solvability in certain cases, rewarding the users in a varied way;

• $EVAA Tokens may be staked, or locked, either directly or indirectly (through LP tokens) to activate eligibility to receive further platform or protocol rewards;

• Boosted APY: Users can earn additional $EVAA rewards on top of their basic supply/borrow APY of other tokens;

In conclusion, $EVAA’s governance and utility features encourage users and token holders to actively participate in the protocol’s governance and liquidity activities in EVAA’s ecosystem.
