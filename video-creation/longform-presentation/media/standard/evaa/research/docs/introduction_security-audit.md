## Quantstamp Audit

Quantstamp’s audit confirmed that EVAA provides TON users with a secure and reliable solution for decentralized lending.

This close collaboration led to a strengthened and more transparent system. Notably, administrative functions in EVAA are now controlled through a multi-signature contract, minimizing the risk of misuse.&#x20;
