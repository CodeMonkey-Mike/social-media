# Ventures

## Seed Round

**Animoca Ventures**\
Part of the Animoca Brands family, Animoca Ventures invests in cutting-edge blockchain projects across gaming, metaverse, and DeFi. Their support brings both capital and ecosystem-level synergy to EVAA.

**TON Ventures**\
The official venture arm of the TON ecosystem, supporting strategic investments that grow and enhance TON's infrastructure. Their backing ensures deep alignment with the TON roadmap.

**Polymorphic Capital**\
A forward-thinking crypto-native fund backing high-conviction teams across early-stage Web3. Their participation in EVAA's seed round reflects confidence in the future of DeFi on TON.

**PAKA**\
Founded in 2020, this fund focuses on Web3 startups, with investments in more than 100 projects aimed at fostering mass adoption of Web3 technologies.

**Mythos Ventures**\
A boutique investment firm focused on decentralization and consumer-grade Web3 experiences. Their selective portfolio emphasizes long-term value creation, making EVAA a strategic fit.

**Frens Syndicate x VC DAO**\
A syndicate comprising Web3 founders, C-level executives, and VC analysts. They provide a collaborative platform for investing in Web3.

## Pre-seed Round

**First EVAAngelist**\
Our first private angel investor owned a network of Telegram channels with a coverage of more than 20 million subscribers. This is not just smart money. It is utility capital.

**TONStarter Angel Round**\
TONStarter is a platform dedicated to launching promising projects in the TON ecosystem. With a keen eye on innovation and scalability, TONStarter's association with EVAA signals a bright future for our initiatives.

**TON Coin Fund**\
TON Coin Fund is a leading syndicate with a $250M portfolio that fosters projects on The Open Network (TON). With their focus on strong teams and visionary projects, their investment in EVAA was a testament to our potential and promise.

**Existential Capital**\
Existential Capital, known for its strategic investments in startups, provided us with funds and invaluable insights into the market. Their faith in us during our pre-seed stage fortified our mission and vision.

**WAGMI Ventures**\
WAGMI Ventures champions Web3 initiatives, investing in trailblazers like Embed.xyz and RLTY.live. Beyond funding, they co-organize events like the Paris Blockchain Week Summit and are members of the French Bored Ape Club. A leading gateway into the Web3 realm, they welcome collaborations and insights.
