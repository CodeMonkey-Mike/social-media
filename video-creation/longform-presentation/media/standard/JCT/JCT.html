What Janction is

A GPU Marketplace From Jasmy

The pitch: an "AI Layer 2"

How It Works

  • GPU owners pool their cards into one network
  • AI and 3D teams rent that compute on demand
  • Validators check the work: proof of the GPU supplied and of the job done
  • JCT: stake it as veJCT for priority in the marketplace, pay with it, vote with it
Tokyo, Japan

Who's Behind It

  • Incubated by Jasmy, the IoT data company founded by ex-Sony executives
  • Run by JasmyLab Inc., CEO Hiroshi Harada, who is also Jasmy's CFO
  • Seed backers named by the project: DWF Labs, Cogitent Ventures, MH Ventures, Waterdrip Capital and others

Launched November 10, 2025 on Binance Alpha and Binance Futures. The token lives on Ethereum and BNB Chain.
