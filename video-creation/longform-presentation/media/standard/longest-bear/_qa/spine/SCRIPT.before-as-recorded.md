# longest-bear: SCRIPT

_The scripted standard video (`video-creation/longform-presentation/skills/script/script.md`): "The Longest Crypto Bear
Market Ever". Both sides of the argument, then what I think. Every number comes from `RESEARCH.md` in this folder (measured
from Bitcoin's daily closes) and `research/macro-claims.md` (each fact with its URL). The opinion at the end is your own
tweet, 2026-11-25. Target: around 5 minutes (822 spoken words)._

## CH3 - SIDE TWO: TWO BEAR MARKETS

💬 `[NOTE]` On screen: the deck slide TWO BEAR MARKETS?
🖥️ `[SCREENSHARE]` Now the other side. Count it with the rule I use, and this is two bear markets, not one.
💬 `[NOTE]` Switch OBS to the full-camera scene in the pause before this line, and back to the screen share after it. Talk it through in your own words; the line below is the content to hit, not a text to read.
👤 `[FACE]` Here's the rule. A bear market is over when Bitcoin goes above its 50-week moving average, the average of the last 50 weekly closes, and its 200-day moving average, the average of the last 200 daily closes, and stays above both for a month and a half.
🗣️ `[COVER]` The first bear market started at that top, on October 6, 2025. We knew we were officially in a bear market when Bitcoin closed below the 50-week moving average two weeks in a row, the weeks ending November 16 and November 23.
🗣️ `[COVER]` It bottomed on June 30, 2026. That's 267 days from the top, and a 53 percent fall.
🗣️ `[COVER]` Then Bitcoin rallied 48 percent, to around 86 thousand dollars by September 21.
🗣️ `[COVER]` And from September 18 through November 7, it stayed above both averages. That's 51 days, a month and a half. By the rule, that bear market was over. Just keep in mind that two months ago, everybody was calling for the bear market to be over, and we're in a new bull market.
