The Problem with Centralized Control

Powers Governments Have Over Your Money
  • Freeze accounts
  • Print unlimited money
  • Impose capital controls
  • Confiscate assets
  • Negative interest rates
The 2008 Financial Crisis — A Turning Point
Banks Failed. Governments Bailed Them Out.
Ordinary people lost homes and savings. The banks that caused the crisis were deemed "too big to fail" and rescued with public money. This is the event that directly inspired the creation of Bitcoin.
Satoshi's Message
Embedded in Bitcoin's first block: "Chancellor on brink of second bailout for banks." Not an accident. A statement of intent.

Chapter 09 — The Setup

Why Bitcoin Was Created

All roads led to one question:
Can we build money
that nobody controls?

Money that can't be printed into worthlessness. That can't be frozen or confiscated. That doesn't require trust in banks, governments, or any single institution. Money governed by math, not by people.

  • No central authority — rules enforced by code
  • Fixed supply — only 21 million Bitcoin will ever exist
  • Transparent — every transaction publicly verifiable
  • Permissionless — anyone, anywhere can participate
  • Censorship resistant — no one can block your transaction
