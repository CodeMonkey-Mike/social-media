Chapter 05

What is Fiat Money?

How It Works
Backed by government decree, not a physical asset
Central banks control its creation and supply
Governments can print more whenever needed
Legal tender laws force its acceptance
Who Controls It
Federal Reserve (USD) — USA
European Central Bank (EUR) — Eurozone
Bank of Japan (JPY) — Japan
A handful of officials set policy for billions
Lecture Script
Fiat is Latin for "let it be done." Money with value by decree — backed by nothing physical.

Chapter 08

The Problem with Centralized Control

Powers Over Your Money
  • Freeze accounts
  • Print unlimited money
  • Impose capital controls
  • Confiscate assets
  • Negative interest rates
2008 — The Turning Point
Too Big To Fail
Banks caused the crisis. Ordinary people lost homes and savings. The banks were bailed out with public money.
Satoshi's Message
Embedded in Bitcoin's first block: "Chancellor on brink of second bailout for banks." A statement of intent.
Lecture Script
Freeze accounts. Print forever. In 2008, the trust finally broke — and Bitcoin was the answer.
