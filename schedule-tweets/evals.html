The clip-strategist: what it proposed, what was kept or killed at the gates, and the score loading…

Where it stands

Score from the gates, by prompt version

Selection precision is the share of proposed clips kept at the first gate (4b), which judges the choice of clip. Final precision counts only clips that also survived the second gate, where a kill may be about the edit. Recall needs a miss review at the gate (--missed "..." or --missed none). A new prompt version appears here on its own after the next livestream batch.

Replays: old transcripts re-run under a later prompt

Run-to-run noise (the same prompt, run more than once on one batch)

A new pick counts as the same clip when it shares at least 50% of the shorter clip's seconds. A pick with no verdict on record is neither right nor wrong, so it is left out of precision. Carry killed material counts picks that match a kept clip (or nothing) yet include at least 10 seconds of a killed one; they do not lower precision, so read them in the batch's replay list. A prompt edit only counts when it moves the score by more than the spread between runs of the same prompt. Same picks between runs pairs the picks one to one, so an extra impact clip counts as a difference.

Batches and decisions

Read-only. Verdicts are made at the gates and written to video-creation/livestream-repurpose/evals/clip-verdicts.jsonl. Open a batch to see every clip, the strategist's reasoning, any misses, and its replays.

Score history

A line is saved each time clip_evals.py score gives a result that differs from the last one.
